## 2. What the AD literature says about our five problems

- **Patch resolution (R and S).** Memory-bank and student-teacher AD scores patches at stride 4 to 16 px (DINO ViT /14 or /16, WideResNet layer2 and layer3). A 1 to 2 px crack lights up a band of 8 to 30 px. So an AD map alone cannot reach our crack IoU (0.47) or relF1@2 (0.88). D07 reached the same conclusion. The useful role is as a *side input* that tells a full-resolution segmenter "something here differs from normal", while the image skip path gives the exact position. CrackCue (R, read here, section 3.3) does exactly this with an image-level residual and gains +2.3 to +6.8 ODS points cross-dataset on 260 training images; under contrast perturbation, U-Net +17.4%. That is the closest evidence that "residual as extra input" helps thin structures under domain shift.
- **Residual features generalise across classes (R).** WeakREST (TIP 2026) feeds the residual between a test feature and its nearest normal feature into a Swin segmenter and beats pixel-supervised methods on MVTec AD with box labels (AP 87.1 vs 86.0). ResAD (NeurIPS 2024) and ResAD++ (IJCV 2026) train on residual features so that one model detects anomalies in classes never seen in training, given a few normal shots. InCTRLv2 (2026) does the same with in-context residuals. S: E1 is a "new class" problem (half-cut layout, scuff); residuals against same-layout normal references are the AD answer to it.
- **Distribution shift (R).** VAND 4.0 (CVPR 2026) industrial track ranks AD under illumination shift on MVTec AD 2. SuperADD (training-free DINOv3 memory bank + intensity augmentation + coverage subsampling + morphological closing) reaches segmentation F1 62.6 / 57.4 / 54.4 on public / private / private-mixed test. S: this is the regime of E1 (new modules, new layout, same sensor family).
- **Weak, low-contrast defects (R).** GLASS targets "weak defects very similar to normal regions" with feature-level gradient-ascent synthesis and validates on woven fabric. 7% of our crack components are less than 2% darker than their surroundings (E).
- **Contaminated pool (R).** All normal-only AD assumes a clean training set. Our 11.9k pool holds defective cells. CoMet (ICCV 2025) and FUN-AD (WACV 2025) train on uncurated data and report no loss from contamination on MVTec AD, VIADUCT, KSDD2 (CoMet). S: needed for any pool-trained AD model here; the cheap alternative is to keep only pool cells the house ensemble calls clean.
- **Scuff (S).** Any normal model trained only on full cells will flag E1 scuff as anomalous, the same as cracks. Two outs: include the 1,152 pool half-cells (scuffs are common there, BRIEF section 2) in the normal bank, so scuff becomes "normal"; or use references from the same module. Without one of these, an AD prior raises E1 false alarms as much as recall.
- **No paper** in this search measures AD maps against 1 to 2 px jittered labels, nor fuses an AD prior into a supervised multi-class segmenter and reports per-class gains. The closest are CrackCue (crack only, binary) and WeakREST (binary defect).

## 4. Cheapest decisive experiments

Instrument for all: module-grouped 5-fold CV over the 532 train + val cells (fold roots exist) and F51; clean-49 test and E1 read once at the end. 100% labels. Five seeds where a gain under 0.02 is claimed.

1. **#1 residual-feature injection (WeakREST-style).** Offline first: build banks (2 GPU-h). Check on F51 and CV val folds the fraction of crack components >= 20 px that the PF ensemble misses (p < 0.001) and that sit in the top 1% of residual norm. Kill offline if < 30%. Then arm: PF + residual at 1/4 and 1/8; baseline: PF. Metrics: CV crack relF1@2 and inactive IoU, then E1 crack relF1@2 and recall in the 20 scuff half-cut cells. Kill: E1 relF1@2 gain < +0.03 and CV inactive gain < +0.02, or CV crack IoU drops > 0.01. Add a "bank without half-cells" arm to test the scuff argument. About 40 GPU-h.
2. **#2 SuperADD prior.** Offline map on F51, H36, CV folds (< 3 GPU-h). Same offline kill as #1. If it passes, fuse as input channel to PF; kill as #1. Compare against the D07 AnomalyDINO / MuSc offline arm in the same table.
3. **#3 ResAD.** Offline only: residual map on half-cut cells (H36 and val half-cut pool cells with house predictions as reference). Kill: half-cut crack component recall at 1% area < the #1 bank map. About 5 GPU-h.
4. **#4 DeSTSeg member.** Arm: 5 PF seeds + DeSTSeg-EL map as a 6th member (averaged on crack and inactive logits only). Baseline: 5 PF seeds. Metric: CV relR@2 at relP@2 >= 0.90, inactive IoU. Kill: relR@2 gain < +0.02. About 12 GPU-h.
5. **#5 GLASS auxiliary head.** Arm: house + GAS hard-feature head (weight 0.1). Baseline: house. Metric: CV crack recall on components < 2% contrast (needs a contrast tag per component, computable from images), crack IoU. Kill: low-contrast recall gain < +0.05 or crack IoU drop > 0.01. About 30 GPU-h.
6. **#6 reference-conditioned U-Net++.** Arm: PF with reference branch; baseline: PF. Metric: E1 crack relF1@2, CV crack IoU. Kill: E1 gain < +0.03 or CV crack IoU drop > 0.012. First check that E1 modules have other cells in the pool (gap below). About 40 GPU-h.
7. **#7 to #10, #12, #15 benchmark AD track.** Train each once on the same clean-pool split (or CoMet-wrapped for #11), report pixel AUROC, AU-PRO, crack and inactive relF1@2, cell-level AUROC, crack recall by length bin (#13). Run through the #14 harness. No kill line; this is suite content. About 30 GPU-h total.
8. **#11 CoMet.** Arm: Dinomaly2 trained on the raw pool with CoMet; baseline: Dinomaly2 on house-clean pool. Metric: inactive and crack-band pixel AUROC on CV. Kill: no difference > 1 AUROC point, then use the simpler filtered pool. About 8 GPU-h.

## 5. Notable papers rejected

- FUN-AD (doi:10.1109/wacv61041.2025.00915): same job as CoMet (#11); CoMet is newer, model-agnostic, and reports KSDD2.
- SoftPatch+ (doi:10.1016/j.patcog.2024.111295): memory-bank denoising, already proposed in D07 for AnomalyDINO.
- SimpleNet (doi:10.1109/cvpr52729.2023.01954): superseded by SuperSimpleNet (D07) and GLASS (#5), same feature-noise idea.
- RealNet (doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.01580), PBAS (doi:10.1109/tcsvt.2024.3479887), CRAS (doi:10.1109/tii.2025.3575122): synthesis variants; GLASS is the representative with code and weak-defect evidence.
- DRA (doi:10.1109/cvpr52688.2022.00724), AHL (doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.01668), DPDL (doi:10.1109/cvpr52734.2025.01901), Mixture Prototype Flow Matching (arxiv:2605.02438): open-set supervised AD scored at image level; we have full pixel labels.
- UniNet (doi:10.1109/cvpr52734.2025.00934): unified unsupervised / supervised AD, abstract gives no pixel evidence relevant to thin defects; candidate extra benchmark row only.
- PromptMAD (doi:10.1109/icassp55912.2026.11464635): supervised segmentor on reconstruction errors, MVTec only, CLIP text prompts unproven on grayscale EL.
- InCTRLv2 (arxiv:2604.04632), Res2CLIP (arxiv:2605.16171), DictAS (doi:10.1109/iccv51701.2025.01908): generalist few-shot AD driven by CLIP semantics; ResAD (#3) covers the residual idea without text.
- AdaCLIP, AD-DINOv3 (doi:10.1109/icassp55912.2026.11464463, code not released per WebSearch), MRAD, FB-CLIP, MultiADS: zero-shot CLIP / DINOv3 variants; AnomalyCLIP (#15) stands in for the family.
- Anomaly-guided weakly supervised OCT lesion segmentation (doi:10.1016/j.media.2024.103139): no abstract in any index; unverified mechanism.
- MambaAD, VarAD, RcAE, CostFilter-AD: multi-class or high-res UAD variants without a distinct mechanism for our five problems.
- Bozic 2021 mixed supervision (doi:10.1016/j.compind.2021.103459): owned by D40.
- CrackCue (doi:10.1016/j.patcog.2025.112107): scored by D08, D09, D12; it is the image-level twin of #1 and should run in the same batch as #1 so the two residual priors are compared head to head.

## 6. Gaps

- Whether the E1 modules have other cells in the 11.9k or 159k pool (needed for same-module references in #3 and #6, and for MuSc in D07). Check the pool's module ids against E1 module ids. If not, references fall back to same-source half-cells.
- No snippet-level evidence (Semantic Scholar rate-limited): I could not check how memory-bank AD behaves on scratch-like thin defects (MVTec "scratch" sub-types, MVTec AD 2 small defects) beyond abstracts.
- WeakREST full text not read: whether PosFAR uses position-aware matching that would fit a fixed cell layout is not checked (fetch arXiv 2306.03492 in the next phase).
- No paper fuses an AD prior into a multi-class (24-class) segmenter. Effect on non-defect classes (gridline, splice) is unknown; #1 should report mIoU_rob.
- Official code for PRN, NDP-Net, GRNR, ResAD (repo not opened), AD-DINOv3 (not released), EfficientAD (only anomalib) not confirmed.
- AD on thermal or EL-IR pairs: only Dinomaly2 mentions RGB-IR; no PV work found.
