## EL facts that decide every adaptation (from BRIEF, E)
- Crack = dark line about 4 px FWHM, label 1 to 2 px, jittered; strict IoU is near a labelling ceiling. Modules that only sharpen localisation cannot pass the ceiling; judge them on relF1@2, relR@2, components found, and E1.
- EL cells contain other thin dark lines that are not cracks: gridline fingers (axis-aligned, periodic, class "gridline", test IoU 0.283, recall 0.33), busbar edges, and on E1 long dark scuff streaks. Any "dark thin ridge" prior (vesselness, CrackCue, LIOT, snake convs) will fire on all of them. This is a risk for crack precision and a chance for gridline recall.
- Cells are axis-aligned (augmentation is rot90 / flips only), so horizontal and vertical strip kernels match fingers, busbars, ribbons, cell borders and splice exactly; cracks have any orientation.
- E1 gap: 70% of E1 crack pixels get p < 0.001; half-cut cells and scuff are absent from training. Contrast / layout invariant inputs and test-time adaptation target this.

## Per-paper detail (R / adaptation / minimal experiment / prior overlap)

1. **CrackCue.** R: U-Net on CrackLS315 ODS 0.790; + coarse cue only 0.833; + full CrackCue 0.847; pooling kernel 8 best (4: 0.825, 12: 0.819); median-filter and black-hat cues gave smaller gains; tested as plug-in on three crack nets, cross-dataset and perturbed images. Min experiment: house + coarse cue channel (zero learnable params), 5 seeds, 20k steps; compare with house 5 seeds on val + F51, then test clean-49 and E1. Metrics: crack relF1@2, crack IoU, gridline IoU, E1 crack relF1@2. Kill: E1 crack relF1@2 gain < 0.03 and test relF1@2 not up, or test crack IoU down > 0.012. GPU about 8 h. Prior overlap: none; "intensity normalisation (fail)" was an inference-time normalisation, not a cue channel.
2. **FR-UNet + DTI.** R: FR-UNet best Sen / AUC / F1 / IoU on DRIVE, CHASE_DB1, STARE, DCA1, CHUAC with fewer parameters (DRIVE F1 0.8316 per a benchmark listing, web); DTI raises sensitivity and connectivity. Min experiment A (0 GPU-h): apply DTI to saved val logits of the 5-seed house and PF ensembles; choose t_high / t_low on val; report test relR@2 at matched relP@2 vs the current logit-bias operating point. Kill: relR@2 gain < 0.01 at equal relP@2. Experiment B: FR-UNet 1-ch from scratch, 5 seeds; kill if single-model crack relF1@2 < house - 0.02 and adding it to the ensemble does not raise ensemble relF1@2. Prior overlap: logit bias and per-module thresholds tested (global shift); hysteresis on connectivity was not.
3. **LIOT.** R: in-dataset slightly below baseline on retina, competitive on CrackTree; cross retinal datasets better mean and lower variance on most metrics, higher sensitivity and connectivity; retina-to-crack transfer works. Min experiment: house with input [EL, LIOT 4 ch], PF variant too (E1 inactive depends on PF), 5 seeds. Metric: E1 crack relF1@2 and recall; test crack IoU as guard. Kill: E1 relF1@2 gain < 0.03 or test crack IoU drop > 0.015. GPU about 12 h. Prior overlap: intensity normalisation at inference failed; LIOT is a training-time representation, still open.
4. **DSCNet.** R: DRIVE Dice 82.06, clDice 82.07; Massachusetts Roads at most +1.31 Dice, +0.77 clDice over U-Net; coronary CTA +3.40 Dice; DSConv stays on the structure where deformable conv wanders. S: the gains on 2D are small; on jittered 1 to 2 px labels the strict IoU will not move much, so measure detection and E1. Min experiment: U-Net++ with DSConv in decoder stages 1/1 and 1/2, house and PF, 5 seeds; kill if crack relF1@2 and components >= 20 px found both within noise (< 0.01) and E1 relF1@2 < +0.02. GPU about 15 h (DSConv is slower). Prior overlap: none (BRIEF: snake / deformable never tried). Bench: most cited tubular module; any thin-structure EL benchmark should include it.
5. **Vesselness inputs (Garret).** R: U-Net on vesselness volumes improved segmentation, mostly small vessels, on two datasets. Contrary evidence (HarmonySeg ablation): plain concatenation of CT + vesselness gave no notable gain on hepatic vessels; attention fusion did. Min experiment: house with [EL, 4 ridge maps]; 5 seeds. Kill: crack relF1@2 and E1 relF1@2 both < +0.01. GPU 7 h. Prior overlap: none.
6. **DconnNet.** R: CVPR 2023, better than SOTA on several medical sets; in the FMS2 re-run on crack sets it had the best Betti error on CRACK500 and best clDice on XCAD (FMS2 Table 1). Min experiment: add connectivity head for 3 thin / rare classes to house; 5 seeds; kill if components >= 20 px found and relF1@2 not up by 0.01. GPU 8 h. Prior overlap: none (connectivity heads never tried; skeleton losses are a different mechanism).
7. **CoANet.** R: road extraction, connectivity improvements on DeepGlobe / SpaceNet (abstract; numbers not read). Min experiment: 4-direction strip-conv decoder in U-Net++, 5 seeds, metric gridline IoU, splice IoU, mIoU_rob, crack relF1@2. Kill: gridline IoU gain < 0.03 and mIoU_rob gain < 0.005. GPU 8 h. Prior overlap: none.
8. **SPDCN.** R: NEU-Seg mIoU 89.6 with 3.54M params (abstract). Min experiment: ADADC in 2 decoder stages, 5 seeds; kill as row 4. GPU 12 h. Prior overlap: none.
9. **W-Net.** R: tiny U-Net close to SOTA; W-Net reaches SOTA on several vessel sets with orders of magnitude fewer weights; cross-dataset drops large; simple self-labelling gave moderate cross-domain gain. Min experiment: frozen house model, train second-stage small U-Net on [EL, softmax] for 10k steps, 5 seeds; kill if ensemble-matched comparison (house 2-model ensemble vs cascade) shows crack relF1@2 gain < 0.01. GPU 6 h. Prior overlap: none (refinement networks never tried). Bench: strong argument and template for a fair "careful simple baseline" in the benchmark.
10. **TMAL.** R: best Dice, IoU, clDice on DRIVE and DeepCrack, lower Betti-0 error (abstract, Nov 2026). Min experiment: soft multi-offset affinity head for crack, 5 seeds; kill as row 6. GPU 10 h. Prior overlap: offset-tolerant CE (n.d.) is a loss on the mask, not affinity prediction; open.
11. **HarmonySeg.** R: hepatic vessels: GSB loss lifts nnU-Net Dice 60.15 to 64.00 (each term +1.1 to +2.9); external set beats DSC-Net and Skeleton Recall nnU-Net in 3 of 4 strata. Min experiment: house + relaxed supervision (no background penalty within 3 px of crack labels) + mixup-equivalence term, 5 seeds; kill if relR@2 gain < 0.01 at equal relP@2. GPU 8 h. Prior overlap: offset-tolerant CE (n.d.) is close to the relaxed term; the mixup-equivalence and reconnection terms are new. Score reduced for that partial overlap.
12. **Strip R-CNN.** R: DOTA-v1.0 82.75 mAP with 30M params (detection, not segmentation). Min experiment: strip context block, 5 seeds; kill if gridline IoU and mIoU_rob not up (thresholds as row 7). GPU 8 h. Prior overlap: none.
13. **Hessian vector field.** R: better generalisation across modalities (fundus to OCTA) than DG baselines (abstract via web; numbers not read). Min experiment: house + Hessian channels + style aug, PF, 5 seeds; kill on E1 relF1@2 < +0.03. GPU 12 h. Prior overlap: none.
14. **TopoTTA.** R: +31.81% average clDice across 4 shift scenarios, 10 datasets, plug-in for CNN TSS models. Min experiment: adapt PF model on 1,152 multi half-cut pool cells, score E1 once; kill if E1 crack relF1@2 gain < 0.03. GPU 10 h (implementation time is the cost: no code). Prior overlap: per-panel BN adaptation failed (BN statistics only); this adds learnable difference convs and pseudo-break consistency, so open.
15. **Dynamic snake upsampling.** R: plug-in gains across datasets and backbones (abstract; no numbers read). Min experiment as row 4. GPU 10 h. Prior overlap: none.
16. **FreeCOS.** R: beats unsupervised and self-supervised methods on XCAD, DRIVE, STARE, CrackTree. Min experiment: pretrain-then-fine-tune, or add fractal cells as a 10% minibatch share; kill on E1 relF1@2 < +0.03 or more E1 false alarms on scuff. GPU 15 h. Prior overlap: crack copy-paste onto pool cells (n.d., more decoys) is the nearest run; differences are procedural shapes, FDA and order alignment. Partly closed, scored low.
17. **FMS2.** R: SegFlow mean mIoU 0.511 to 0.599 over 5 crack / vessel sets vs Mask2Former, DconnNet, FlowSDF, Topograph, SCSegamba; trained on 400 crack patches at 256 px; SynFlow recovers near-full performance with 25% labels. S: baselines look under-tuned (DRIVE F1 0.72 to 0.78 vs about 0.82 usual), so effect sizes are inflated. Moonshot. GPU 30 h. Prior overlap: none.
18. **Pitfalls.** Benchmark only; no training experiment. Use it to fix component connectivity, metric choice and GT artifact handling in the EL benchmark protocol.

## Notable papers rejected
- TopoLoRA-SAM (arXiv 2601.02273): SAM + LoRA for thin structures; its own DRIVE / CHASE Dice (0.57 to 0.60) is far below standard U-Net values, so the evidence is weak; foundation adapters belong to another direction.
- UCS (arXiv 2504.04034): SAM-based universal curvilinear model; SAM adapters are covered by the foundation-model direction.
- Few-shot annotation correction for retinal vessels (doi:10.3389/fmed.2026.1682878): read in full; it is a noise-injection study on a plain U-Net (sensitivity falls 31% at high positional noise) with no correction method we can reuse.
- DSCformer (doi:10.22260/isarc2025/0140): DSConv + SegFormer dual branch for concrete cracks, Crack3238 IoU 59.2; code not verified; test DSConv alone first (row 4).
- GLCP (doi:10.1007/978-3-032-05325-1_23): skeleton + discontinuity heads; close to Skeleton Recall lines already run; code only promised.
- Centerline Boundary Dice, clDice, Skeleton Recall, coDice: loss direction; clDice and Skeleton Recall already run here.
- SCSegamba (doi:10.1109/cvpr52734.2025.02738) and MixerCSeg: crack-specific Mamba nets; belong to the crack-architecture direction but SCSegamba should be in the benchmark suite (CVPR 2025, 104 citations).
- Most DSConv citers (YOLO + DSConv detectors, about 20 of top 30 citers): detection add-ons with no segmentation ablation.
- Lightweight retinal nets (LFRA-Net, DSAE-Net 78K params, Lvs-Net, U-PEN Mamba): target parameter count, not accuracy on hard data.
- Remote-sensing road nets in MDPI / JSTARS (DPSDA-Net, FERDNet, MSPNet, AGF-Net, TopoRF-Net): incremental strip or attention variants of CoANet, no code checked, no small-data evidence.
- CurvSegFlow, Flow matching 3D curvilinear (2026): microscopy / 3D, same idea as FMS2.
- FSG-Net (doi:10.1186/s12880-025-02021-4): useful reproducibility re-implementations, but the mechanism (guided filter attention) is a generic decoder tweak.
- Fractal feature maps (arXiv 2407.14754): no abstract retrieved; not assessed.

## Gaps
- search_snippets was rate-limited all session, so ablation numbers for FR-UNet, CoANet, SPDCN, Strip R-CNN and the Hessian vector field paper were not read; table values from abstracts only.
- Code for TopoTTA, CrackCue, HarmonySeg not public at time of search; DSCNet repo URL from a web summary, not opened.
- No paper found that tests these modules on 1 to 2 px jittered labels or reports tolerant (relaxed-F1) metrics; our EL runs would be the first evidence.
- Neuron-specific modules (EM / light microscopy) were mostly 3D instance methods with no 2D small-data value; not pursued.
- Sub-direction worth its own search: "crack cue" style background-reconstruction priors for EL (inpaint a defect-free cell from the pool, use the residual) overlaps with anomaly-detection priors, which BRIEF lists as never tried.
- Sub-direction: hysteresis / connectivity-aware post-processing on saved logits (DTI) is a zero-GPU check that the inference team can run first.
