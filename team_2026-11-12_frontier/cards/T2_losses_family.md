## 0. What the family has to work with (E, BRIEF 2, 3, 5; PROTOCOL 5)
- Crack labels: 1 to 2 px, median 1.75 px, half of label points > 1 px off the darkest ridge; a 1 px shift of a perfect mask gives IoU 0.55; two drawings agree at IoU 0.21 to 0.26 but 0.82 to 0.99 within 2 px. So any loss that insists on exact pixel placement fights the labels.

## 1. TopoMortar (rank 24): the evidence base for topology losses
- **Id / links:** arXiv:2503.03365, https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03365 ; project https://jmlipman.github.io/TopoMortar
- **Claims (R, full text, Tables 2 and 3):** nnU-Net in MONAI, 12k iterations, batch 10, SGD 0.01, 10 seeds, paired permutation tests. Eight losses (CEDice, RegionWise, TopoLoss, TOPO, Warping, clDice, SkelRecall, cbDice). Large accurate training set: every loss ties CEDice on Dice (0.91 ID); only clDice and SkelRecall cut Betti errors significantly (ID beta0 1.17 and 3.08 vs 3.31; OOD 81 and 180 vs 197). Noisy labels: SkelRecall best topology (ID beta0 3.8 vs CEDice 6.2), clDice second. Small set and pseudo-labels: clDice best. Self-distillation (train 4k iterations, relabel the training set with the model's soft output, retrain, twice) lowers Betti errors for most losses, e.g. noisy + SD CEDice beta0 6.2 -> 2.4. Their conclusion: augmentation and self-distillation lift plain CEDice above most topology losses. Cost: Warping 152 h vs CEDice 2.3 h.
- **Scout figure check:** the scout quoted "CrackTree Dice: CEDice 0.78, cbDice 0.76, clDice 0.54, SkelRecall 0.32". I did not find these numbers in the retrieved text (the fetch covered 100k of 104k characters). Unverified; not used.
- **Evidence check (S):** mortar lines are 5 to 20 px wide with clean geometry, not 1.75 px jittered traces. The transferable findings: (1) topology losses do not raise overlap on a large clean set; (2) under noisy labels the cheap recall-type losses (SkelRecall, clDice) gave the topology gains, which matches our E1 SKR result; (3) self-distillation is a strong, cheap baseline under label noise. The topology metrics they use (Betti errors) are not our headline; crack component recall is the nearest of ours.
- **Code:** project page; repository and licence not opened (unverified).
- **Hypothesis for us (S):** self-distillation of soft crack/inactive maps turns the 1 px jitter into soft targets learned from the model itself; tested in arm `ens_kd_jml` with an ensemble teacher (stronger than one previous round).
- **Benchmark value (S):** high. Adopt its protocol habits: 10 seeds for final claims where affordable, paired tests, a topology / component error printed beside overlap, the cost column.
- **Status: RUN_AFTER_GATE** (arm `ens_kd_jml`; gate G5 in the spec: wave-1 PF fold runs exist and their 2-seed out-of-fold ensemble beats a single seed).

## 2. Jaccard Metric Losses, JML (rank 8)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.52202/075280-3289, arXiv:2302.05666 (NeurIPS 2023); code https://github.com/zifuwanggg/JDTLosses (opened).
- **Claims (R, full text):** JML1 = 1 - (|x|+|y|-|x-y|)/(|x|+|y|+|x-y|) (L1 norms) equals the soft Jaccard loss on hard labels and, unlike soft Jaccard / soft Dice, is a metric on [0,1]^n, so it is minimised at prediction = soft target. CE/JML weights 0.25/0.75 used throughout (VOC DL3-R18: CE alone 72.47, 0.10/0.90 73.67, 0.25/0.75 74.42, 0.50/0.50 74.30). Gains: Cityscapes DL3-R18 72.88 -> 75.55; VOC 72.47 -> 74.42; ADE20K SegFormer-B0 30.49 -> 36.65, B5 48.17 -> 49.95. Boundary label smoothing best at k 3, eps 0.5; BLS adds +0.48 mIoU with CE but +1.26 with JML (VOC DL3-R101). KD: CE 72.47 -> +JML 74.42 -> +JML-KD 75.05 -> JML-BLS teacher 75.89 (VOC DL3-R18). Active classes for KD: LABEL 75.89 > ALL 75.21 > PRESENT 74.50. Warnings: per-GPU small batches optimise per-image IoU (hurts dataset IoU); class-wise averaging can over-weight very rare classes. JML raises calibration error (ECE 0.68 -> 3.07, Cityscapes).
- **Code check (R, repo page + jdt_loss.py):** PyTorch; `JDTLoss(mIoUD, mIoUI, mIoUC, alpha, beta, gamma, smooth=1e-3, threshold=0.01, norm, log_loss, ignore_index, class_weights, active_classes_mode_hard='PRESENT', active_classes_mode_soft='ALL')`; mIoUD = batch-level, mIoUI = per image. Soft path uses tp = (|p|+|y|-|p-y|)/2. No licence shown on the repo page (unverified): re-implement (20 lines) rather than copy.
- **Evidence check (S):** big gains are on natural-image mIoU with many mid-size classes. On hard labels, JML equals soft Jaccard, which differs from the house soft Dice only by a monotone per-batch rescaling (J = D / (2 - D)); so swapping Dice for JML on hard labels is not worth an arm. JML matters only together with soft targets (smoothing, distillation). Our house Dice is batch-level with smooth 1, which already matches their mIoUD advice.
- **Hypothesis (S):** with soft crack targets that encode the 1 to 2 px jitter, a loss that is minimised at the soft target stops forcing the model to choose one side of the label line; strict IoU and relF1@2 should both rise or hold with no width growth at argmax.
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `soft_band_jml`; also the loss of `ens_kd_jml`).

## 3. Spatially Varying Label Smoothing, SVLS (rank 41)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1007/978-3-030-78191-0_52, arXiv:2104.05788 (IPMI 2021). Code: not found (unverified).
- **Claims (R, abstract only):** soft labels from a Gaussian-weighted local neighbourhood of the one-hot map, so boundary pixels get soft targets and interiors stay hard; better boundary prediction, uncertainty and calibration on 4 clinical tasks; handles multiple raters. No numbers retrieved. Exact kernel (size, sigma, centre weight) unverified.
- **Evidence check (S):** medical blobs, where most pixels are interior. A 1.75 px crack is all boundary, so SVLS reduces to a soft band around the trace. We do not need their exact kernel: we can measure the band from our own two label versions (pre-check P1 in the spec).
- **Hypothesis (S):** a soft crack band of measured strength (crack mass about 0.1 to 0.3 at 1 px, about 0.05 at 2 px, never 0.5 or more) is the right target for jittered thin labels; with JML it should raise strict IoU and relF1@2.
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `soft_band_jml`, which is SVLS-style smoothing restricted to the crack class, built with a Euclidean distance kernel).

## 4. GeoLS, geodesic soft labels (rank 63)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.59275/j.melba.2025-c1d9 (MELBA 2025 journal extension); conference version MIDL 2023 (PMLR v227, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v227/vasudeva24a.html, found by search, not opened). Code https://github.com/adigasu/GeoLS (listed by search, not opened; depends on GeodisTK, absent here).
- **Claims (R, abstract and search snippet only):** soft labels from the geodesic distance transform on image intensity, so smoothing follows intensity and does not cross edges; better Dice and Hausdorff than other soft-labelling methods on public medical sets. No numbers retrieved.
- **Evidence check (S):** fits our case unusually well in principle: the true crack is the dark ridge and half of label points sit > 1 px off it, so mass that flows along dark pixels re-centres the soft label on the ridge. Risk: 7% of crack components are < 2% darker than surroundings, and busbars / gridlines are also dark lines, so geodesic mass can leak along a gridline.
- **Hypothesis (S):** geodesic band beats the Euclidean band of `soft_band_jml` on strict IoU, because it moves target mass onto the ridge the model can see.
- **Status: RUN_AFTER_GATE.** Gate: (1) `soft_band_jml` is not dropped on its screen, and (2) CPU pre-check P1g in the spec (geodesic band puts its mass on darker pixels than the Euclidean band in >= 70% of crack cells). Then it runs as the variant `soft_band_jml_geo` (only the distance changes). Geodesic distance via scipy `csgraph.dijkstra` on a 5 px band, no new package.

## 5. Robust T-Loss (rank 73)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1007/978-3-031-43898-1_68, arXiv:2306.00753 (MICCAI 2023; MedIA 2025 journal version per scout, not opened). Code https://github.com/Digital-Dermatology/t-loss, Apache 2.0, PyTorch (README read; `tloss.py` read).
- **Claims (R, abstract):** NLL of a Student-t with one learnable parameter that sets robustness; beats traditional losses on Dice for ISIC 2017 skin lesions and Shenzhen lungs under simulated label noise (noise levels alpha 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7, beta 0.5 / 0.7 per README). No numbers in abstract or README.
- **Code check (R):** per image, delta = input - target over all D = H x W pixels; loss = -lgamma((nu'+D)/2) + lgamma(nu'/2) + (D/2) log pi + (D/2)(nu+eps) + ((nu'+D)/2) log(1 + sum(delta^2) / nu'), nu' = exp(nu) + eps, nu learnable (init 1.0), lambdas fixed at 1. No activation inside: it expects probabilities and a binary target.
- **Evidence check (S):** the robust weighting acts on the per-image sum of squared errors, so every pixel of an image gets the same scale factor (nu'+D)/(nu' + sum delta^2). It down-weights whole images with large error; it does not down-weight individual mislabelled pixels. Our crack noise is a 1 px displacement present in every crack image, so the mechanism does not target it. On a 0.19% class the underlying MSE on probabilities gives weak gradients.
- **Hypothesis (S):** no expected gain on our headline; a valid robust-loss baseline for the benchmark's method suite (binary crack channel added to the house CE).
- **Status: BENCH_ONLY.** Cheap to run as a benchmark row once the composite loss exists (config + 30 lines), 1 seed x 5 folds.

## 6. RankED (rank 40)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.00312, arXiv:2403.01795 (CVPR 2024); project page https://ranked-cvpr24.github.io (repository and licence not opened, unverified).
- **Claims (R, abstract):** a ranking term that puts positive (edge) pixels above negatives, for imbalance, plus a term that orders positives by label certainty (annotator agreement); new state of the art on NYUD-v2, BSDS500, Multicue. No numbers retrieved.
- **Evidence check (S):** edge maps are scored with ODS / OIS / AP after NMS and thinning; our headline is argmax segmentation. Pixel AP is secondary here because 42% of crack ground truth sits below p 0.001 (E, PROTOCOL 5.1). Pairwise ranking over about 4k positive and 2M negative pixels per batch needs sampling or the aLRP-style sorting trick; cost and memory not checked. A certainty map exists in principle (2021 vs 2022 crack masks).
- **Status: PARKED** (cost and fit: not a priority for the one-month score goal). Reopen if `soft_band_jml` shows that label-uncertainty weighting moves relF1@2; the cheapest decision run is the sorting term alone on crack, 1 seed, fold 0.

## 7. SCNP, same-class neighbour penalisation (rank 34)
- **Id / links:** arXiv:2603.18671 (CVPR 2026); project https://jmlipman.github.io/SCNP-SameClassNeighborPenalization (code licence not stated, unverified).
- **Claims (R, full text incl. appendix tables I.9, I.10):** during training only, for each class channel, a pixel's logit is replaced by the worst logit among same-label neighbours in a w x w window (minimum for pixels of that class, maximum for pixels not of that class); w = 3 default, best w tracks structure thickness (FIVES: w 9 for 9.69 px vessels). Works with CE, Dice, Tversky, focal, clDice, SkelRecall. Numbers (DeepLabv3+ in Detectron2): **Crack500** CEDice Dice 0.68, beta0e 10.61, clDice 0.76; CEDice + CEDice-SCNP 0.68 / 9.32 / 0.76; CEDice-SCNP alone 0.66 / 9.48 / 0.73. **TopoMortar** CEDice 0.67 / 25.06 / 0.75; CEDice + SCNP 0.68 / 20.86 / 0.76. **DeepGlobe roads** CEDice 0.75 / 11.77 / 0.82; + SCNP 0.72 / 7.24 / 0.80 (Dice cost). FIVES (nnU-Net): beta0e 10.82 -> 6.94 at equal Dice 0.92.
- **Evidence check (S):** on the one crack set (Crack500) the gain is topological (beta0 -12%) at equal Dice; the "+SCNP on top of CEDice" form keeps Dice, the replace form loses 0.02. Crack500 labels are wider than ours. With 1 px jitter, the "not-this-class" side of SCNP punishes any crack bleed beside the label line, so it should narrow predictions; the "this-class" side punishes gaps along the line.
- **Hypothesis (S):** fewer broken cracks (component recall up) and less width (relP@2 up), with gridline continuity as a free side effect (gridline IoU 0.283, recall 0.33). Risk: under jitter the model may lower its crack confidence overall (relR@2 down).
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `scnp_cedice`).

## 8. CoLeTra, disconnect-to-connect augmentation (rank 36)
- **Id / links:** arXiv:2503.05541; code https://github.com/jmlipman/CoLeTra (licence not stated in the paper; repo not opened, unverified).
- **Claims (R, full text):** n random foreground pixels are chosen; around each, an s x s Gaussian-weighted blend replaces the image with an inpainted (LaMa, precomputed once, inpainting mask = label dilated 3 times) version, label unchanged. Chosen s, n for Crack500: 19, 19 (DynUNet), 15, 30 (AttentionUNet); n set so 40 to 60% of the structure is covered. Crack500 DynUNet CE: beta0e 9.687 -> 9.135, Dice 69.3 -> 69.7; DRIVE Dice 72.6 -> 72.5; Narwhal (topologically wrong labels) AttentionUNet beta0e 24430 -> 16806, Dice 70.1 -> 72.6. About 1 ms per iteration. Robustness to s, n shown on DRIVE only (authors' stated limitation).
- **Evidence check (S):** small Dice effects, consistent topology effects. For us it targets the faint cracks (7% < 2% contrast) and E1's misses (70% of E1 crack px get p < 0.001) by teaching that a crack label can continue where the image evidence fades. Labels are untouched, so no width bias by construction. LaMa is not installed; OpenCV Telea inpainting is (opencv-headless 5.0), weaker on the finger / busbar texture.
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `coletra_crack`; a data-pipeline arm kept in this family because its evidence and code come from the same topology line as TopoMortar / SCNP).

## 9. Centerline cross-entropy, clCE (rank 70)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1007/978-3-031-72111-3_67 (MICCAI 2024); paper PDF https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/paper/1081_paper.pdf (read); code https://github.com/cesaracebes/centerline_CE (Apache-2.0 per repo page; files not opened). No arXiv version found.
- **Claims (R, full text):** clCE = CE averaged over the target skeleton S_T (Eq. 5) + CE averaged over the soft skeleton of the prediction, weighted by its soft values (Eq. 6, clDice soft-skeleton algorithm). Added with weight 1 to Dice. Tables 1, 2 (2D small U-Net, 512 px patches; 3D nnU-Net): Dice + clCE raises both DSC and clDice everywhere: HRF 78.93 -> 79.34 DSC, 77.46 -> 78.94 clDice; DR-HAGIS 69.22 -> 70.65, 68.73 -> 70.06; TREND 63.92 -> 64.54, 65.32 -> 67.04; ASOCA 84.59 -> 84.80; FIVES-N/A/G/D DSC +0.08 to +0.42, clDice +0.99 to +1.3. Dice + 0.5 clDice lost 1.2 to 5.9 DSC (clDice at weight 1 was unstable, halved). Same trends with CE instead of Dice as base (supplement, not read).
- **Discrepancy (R):** the paper's Code 1 reduces each term with `.mean()` over all pixels, while Eqs. 5 and 6 normalise by the skeleton mass. On a 0.19% class the code version is about 1/500 of the equation version. The spec uses the equation form and says so. The paper's names are also swapped (its "CE-Tprec" averages over the target skeleton, which is a recall term).
- **Evidence check (S):** vessels are 3 to 15 px wide with clean centrelines; gains are small (+0.1 to +1.4 DSC) but in both metrics. Our clDice run failed and Skeleton Recall widened; clCE differs by being CE-shaped (log penalty, the property the authors credit for noise robustness) and by having a prediction-skeleton term that penalises thin false lines. Caveat (S): a wide prediction centred on the label has its skeleton on the label, so the prediction term does not stop widening; width must be guarded.
- **Hypothesis (S):** at a weight between SKR's 0.1 (narrow E1 pass, no false alarms) and 1.0 (widening), clCE lifts crack component recall and relR@2 while its prediction-skeleton term cuts thin false lines (scuff, gridline confusion), so relP@2 holds.
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `clce_crack`).

## 10. DconnNet, directional connectivity (rank 44)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1109/cvpr52729.2023.01109, arXiv:2304.00145 (CVPR 2023); code https://github.com/Zyun-Y/DconnNet (link from the arXiv page; licence not checked).
- **Claims (R, abstract):** a directional connectivity scheme that separates a directional subspace from shared features and uses it through the network; beats state of the art on public medical benchmarks. No numbers or dataset names in the abstract.
- **Evidence check (S):** an architecture + output-representation change, not a loss: 8-neighbour connectivity channels per class, decoded back to a mask. With 1 to 2 px jittered labels the direction targets are noisy (the scout's own D10 note suggests targets from 1 px dilated labels). Build cost about 8 h (head, targets, decoding, a 24-class decision rule), and multi-class support in the official code is unverified.
- **Status: PARKED** (cost; belongs with the architecture family if anywhere). Reopen if `scnp_cedice` or `clce_crack` moves crack component recall, which would show that continuity supervision pays on our labels. Cheapest decision run: an 8-channel BCE connectivity head on crack only beside the 24-way head, fold 0, 1 seed.

## 11. CC-DiceCE, instance-wise loss (rank 74)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1109/isbi61048.2026.11515682, arXiv:2511.17146 (ISBI 2026); code https://github.com/TIO-IKIM/Learning-to-Look-Closer (nnU-Net v2 fork; binary only; CuPy connected components and Voronoi; licence file present, type not read).
- **Claims (R, full text):** components of the ground truth; each gets its Voronoi region (pixels closer to it than to any other component); DiceCE is computed per region and averaged over components, so each lesion counts equally; combined 1:1 with global DiceCE; non-smooth Dice (eps 0) because smoothing destabilised CMB. nnU-Net, 5-fold CV, 5 brain MRI sets. Recall: LAC 0.321 -> 0.370, CMB 0.542 -> 0.578, SBM 0.799 -> 0.812, BraTS 0.451 -> 0.529, WMH 0.656 -> 0.643. Precision up on LAC, CMB; flat on SBM, WMH; BraTS 0.771 -> 0.052 (as extracted; the text confirms a large drop). Dice flat (LAC 0.277 -> 0.266, CMB 0.395 -> 0.425). Beats blob loss generally.
- **Evidence check (S):** our crack is the textbook case of many tiny components (76 to 88% under 20 px); global Dice is dominated by the few long cracks (fold 3 alone holds 100k of 310k crack px, PROTOCOL 3.2). But some "components" are fragments of one physical crack broken by label gaps, so components must be merged across small gaps before weighting. The BraTS precision collapse is a warning for false alarms.
- **Hypothesis (S):** per-component weighting raises crack component recall (>= 20 px and any-hit) and relR@2 on short cracks, with a precision cost to watch on the 303 crack-free CV cells.
- **Status: RUN_NOW** (arm `cc_dicece_crack`).

## 12. Instance awareness of multi-class losses (rank 82)
- **Id / links:** arXiv:2604.24276. Code: none linked (unverified).
- **Claims (R, abstract):** one-vs-rest extension of blob and CC losses to multi-class, classes averaged equally; inverse-size weighting only inside each component. BraTS-METS 2025, 260 test cases: CC loss foreground Dice 0.64 +- 0.26 vs 0.59 +- 0.27; blob loss PQ 0.40 vs 0.38; inverse-size weighting raises rare-class Dice to 0.44 at a detection cost.
- **Evidence check (S):** one dataset, wide intervals. Its extension to several classes is what our inactive (42 train cells, 48 in P532) and gridline would need, but it stacks on the crack-only CC result.
- **Status: RUN_AFTER_GATE.** Gate: `cc_dicece_crack` passes on crack component recall without tripping the precision guard; then the variant `cc_dicece_multi` extends the same term to inactive (11) and gridline (class id as in the R13 list; the harness's class table decides) one-vs-rest.

## 13. Adjusting ground truth with snakes (rank 81)
- **Id / links:** doi:10.1109/tmi.2022.3193072, arXiv:2112.02781 (TMI 2022); code https://github.com/doruk-oner/AdjustingAnnotationswithSnakes (link from arXiv page; not opened).
- **Claims (R, abstract):** annotations treated as active contours that deform during training while keeping topology; network and annotations optimised jointly; aimed at imprecise 3D delineation annotations. No numbers in abstract.
- **Evidence check (S):** a label-refinement method (polyline extraction, snake energy, re-rasterisation per source width), not a loss. ## 15. VOID check
No paper in this family repeats a mechanism already run here under the same conditions. Nearest rows (BRIEF 5, Loss): soft clDice (Dice-shaped skeleton loss; clCE is CE-shaped with a prediction term), Skeleton Recall (recall-only; clCE adds precision), offset-tolerant CE at 10% labels (a min over shifts; soft bands with JML at 100% labels are a different target and loss). Nothing is VOID.
