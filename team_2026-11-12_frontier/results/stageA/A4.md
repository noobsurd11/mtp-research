## 1. Reading rule (fixed before any number; written 04:49, before the tool was run)
- Statistic (critic fix 1): distances between mutually nearest skeleton points of the two drawings of one crack (a pair (a, b) counts when b is a nearest point of a on the other skeleton and a is a nearest point of b; ties count). tau95 = 95th percentile (numpy linear interpolation) of the pooled distances. Skeleton = `bench/skel.py` (the one frozen implementation). The spec's original statistic (mask px within a 10 px cut, both ways) is printed as secondary only.
- Pairs: rows of `benchmark/out/pairs.tsv` whose two stems share a group in `benchmark/out/groups_all.tsv`, BMRK / SDLE skipped (as `label_checks.py` section 3); both stems in the 532 pool (every legacy test and E1 stem is refused before any image or label is read); image alignment by NCC (crop 32 px, shift <= 8 px); v1.1 crack (`masks_v11/masks` with ignore px removed); both drawings >= 100 px; exact copies (raw crack IoU >= 0.999) and near copies (raw crack IoU >= 0.85) excluded; identical masks counted once (pairs of a non-representative copy dropped).
- Growth-affected pair (fixed now): share of skeleton points (both drawings) farther than 10 px from the other skeleton > 0.20, OR skeleton length ratio (longer / shorter) > 1.5.
- Subsets: NG = pairs of two different images that are not growth-affected; DA = the one double annotation `CFVS_00015_r7_c4` / `CFVS_00016_r7_c4` (asserted to be one image: NCC >= 0.999 at shift 0).
- Intervals: 95% percentile interval from 2,000 bootstrap draws (seed 0) over pairs for NG (spec); over 32 px tiles for DA (a single pair cannot be resampled by pairs); over physical cells (group + position) as a secondary for NG.
- Decision (critic fix 2): the default headline tolerance stays @2. It leaves @2 only if the CI low of tau95 is above 2.5 px on NG AND on DA. If it leaves: tau95 (NG) in (2.0, 3.0] gives relF1@3 as headline with @2 beside it (spec band); tau95 above 3.0 has no band in the spec and goes to the lead. The spec's "tau95 <= 2.0 with CI high <= 2.5 confirms @2 with a citable rule" is reported. A move down to @1 is not possible under the critic's rule; if the NG CI high is <= 1.0 the lead decides. Under Y13 (L7) the change, if any, applies to the benchmark table and to arms specified after it; pre-declared primaries keep their tolerance.
- Always reported: unmatched shares at 5, 10, 20 px (growth or missed branches, which no tolerance covers); per source (ARTS, CFVS); inactive boundaries (descriptive only).
