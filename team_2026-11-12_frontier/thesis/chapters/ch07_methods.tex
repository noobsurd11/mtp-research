\chapter{Baselines and methods under the protocol}\label{ch:methods}

This chapter reports every model and method we ran under the benchmark protocol of Chapter~\ref{ch:protocol}, on the 532 pool
cells (P532) only, each cell scored once by a model that did not train on its fold. Nothing in this chapter is a statement about
unseen modules; Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result} gives the one sealed test. The work ran in two tiers. Screens trained 2 seeds on
the older split cv5v2 and could only promote an arm, never establish a result (Section~\ref{sec:m-suite} and
Section~\ref{sec:m-screens}). Rows trained 5 seeds (seeds 5 to 9) on the corrected split cv5v3, in which no physical module
spans two folds, and were read against a rule registered before the read (Sections~\ref{sec:m-confirm} to~\ref{sec:m-final}).
Section~\ref{sec:m-robust} tests the contrasts against label variants, and Section~\ref{sec:m-weak} states what was done for the
weak classes.

The rules are those of Chapter~\ref{ch:protocol}. Crack relF1@2 decides. A crack gain counts only if, at OP-A (argmax) and at
OP-W (a crack bias cross-fitted so that the predicted crack width matches the reference), on the 532 cells and on the 416 clean
cells, the gain is at least the margin M = 0.010 and the lower end of the 95\% two-stage group-bootstrap interval is above 0,
and no guard fails: crack width within 0.85 to 1.15 times the reference, pooled and per source with at least 40 cells; false
crack pixels on crack-free cells at most 1.5 times the reference; mIoU-R13 and inactive IoU not lower by more than their margins.
Strict crack IoU is printed with its convention band $\pm$0.019 and decides nothing. M covers seed noise, not the choice of
folds. All 5-seed rows were trained on one machine; part of the screens were trained on a second machine, and no read mixes
models of the two.

\section{The reference recipes: PF and HOUSE}\label{sec:m-ref}

Both reference recipes use U-Net++ \citep{zhou2018unetpp} with an ImageNet ResNet-34 encoder \citep{he2015resnet}, one input
channel, 512 px, batch 8, Adam at learning rate $10^{-3}$ with a 200-step warm-up and cosine decay, weight decay $10^{-4}$,
20,000 steps, cross-entropy plus soft Dice on crack and inactive, and label-safe augmentation (flips, quarter turns, brightness
and contrast). HOUSE is this recipe with a plain fine-tune. PF, the encoder-preserving fine-tune, freezes the encoder for
1,000 steps, then ramps it to 0.1 times the learning rate over the next 1,000 steps, with no encoder weight decay. PF is the baseline of every contrast:
the plain fine-tune erases the pretrained deep encoder stages, and PF keeps them (Chapter~\ref{ch:legacy}).

On cv5v3 (Table~\ref{tab:T5}; OP-A, mean over seeds 5 to 9) PF reaches crack relF1@2 0.8463 (0.8264 on the 416 clean cells),
strict crack IoU 0.3653, inactive IoU 0.5042 and mIoU-R13 0.6116, with 212 false crack pixels on the crack-free cells and a
width ratio of 1.0189. HOUSE reaches 0.852, 0.383, 0.514 and 0.607, with 774 false crack pixels and a width ratio of 1.13.

HOUSE $-$ PF has no registered rule; its intervals were read afterwards and are printed only (Table~\ref{tab:T6}, last but one
row). On relF1@2 the contrast is +0.0059 [$-$0.0043, +0.0152] at 532 cells and OP-A, and its interval holds 0 at all four values
(+0.0034, +0.0066 and +0.0025 at the other three). Strict crack IoU is +0.0179 [+0.0093, +0.0266], inside the convention band.
Nine guard items are outside a bound: the ARTS width at all four values (1.307 / 1.167 / 1.370 / 1.190), the CFVS width on the
416 cells at OP-A (1.164), and false crack pixels at 2.55 to 3.66 times PF. Our reading (judgement): HOUSE draws cracks wider
and makes more false crack pixels than PF; its strict-IoU edge is within the size a change of line-width convention produces
(Section~\ref{sec:m-robust}), and on relF1@2 it is not separated from PF. HOUSE stays a row of its own, and any arm that claims
an in-distribution crack gain is also printed against it (the HOUSE rule; Section~\ref{sec:m-final}).

\section{The baseline suite: models of the EL papers under equal training}\label{sec:m-suite}

The first screen tranche asks how the model families of published EL segmentation work compare with PF when every row gets the
same data, labels, steps and augmentation (Table~\ref{tab:T15}; cv5v2, 2 seeds against 5 PF seeds, 532 cells, argmax). The
rows are not reproductions of the published numbers: the UCF-EL row uses ImageNet weights rather than COCO weights and no
auxiliary head, and the Pratt row uses our augmentation with the paper's weighted loss. The cv5v2 split carries a module leak
(Chapter~\ref{ch:data}); all arms share it, so contrasts between arms stay valid.

Against PF on crack relF1@2 (Table~\ref{tab:T16a}), U-Net with a ResNet-34 encoder \citep{ronneberger2015unet} is at
$-$0.0016 [$-$0.0110, +0.0073], and the VGG-16 U-Net of \citet{hanifi2025multiclass} at $-$0.0064 [$-$0.0189, +0.0042]: neither
is separated from PF. DeepLabV3+ with ResNet-34 \citep{chen2018deeplabv3plus}, output stride 4, is at $-$0.0093
[$-$0.0204, +0.0019] at argmax and $-$0.0199 [$-$0.0325, $-$0.0086] once its width is matched; it draws cracks 1.21 times as wide
as labelled, which hides part of the loss at argmax. Two models from EL papers sit clearly below PF at both operating points: the
DeepLabV3 / ResNet-50 of UCF-EL \citep{chen2017deeplabv3,fioresi2022ucfel}, output stride 8, at $-$0.0773 [$-$0.0892, $-$0.0594],
and the DeepLabV3+ with the weighted cross-entropy of \citet{pratt2023benchmark} at $-$0.0932 [$-$0.1113, $-$0.0774]. The weighted loss makes
cracks 3.05 times too wide (relP@2 0.648, relR@2 0.892); with the width matched it is still at $-$0.0899 [$-$0.1130, $-$0.0693].

Two readings follow, both judgement. No decoder effect was detected here: plain U-Net is not separated from U-Net++ on relF1@2 ($-$0.0016 [$-$0.0110, +0.0073]). Output stride
does: stride 4 costs about 0.02 relF1@2 once width is matched, and stride 8 about 0.08 to 0.10. Nothing in this tranche beats PF
or HOUSE. The limits are 2 seeds per row, one machine and pool cells of known modules only.

\section{Screens that did not pass}\label{sec:m-screens}

Each further arm took a published idea that targets one of the problems of this data (thin cracks, rare classes, the encoder
fine-tune, generalisation across sources) and adapted it to the PF recipe. Table~\ref{tab:T16a} lists the primary contrast and
the verdict of every arm that did not pass; Table~\ref{tab:T15} gives the levels and Figure~\ref{fig:F10} the contrasts against PF. Two arms passed their screen and were
promoted: HRNet-W18 U-Net++ \citep{wang2021hrnet} (+0.0114 [+0.0032, +0.0203] at OP-A and +0.0128 [+0.0041, +0.0221] at OP-W)
and constant-rate weight averaging after SWAD \citep{cha2021swad} (+0.0135 [+0.0051, +0.0221] at OP-A). A screen pass is a
promotion to a 5-seed row, not a result; Section~\ref{sec:m-confirm} reads both.

Squares: 5-seed rows on cv5v3; circles: 2-seed screens on cv5v2, run on the training machine or a second machine, each read against
its own PF. Open squares (*): CS and CS\_D4, selection-biased. Dashed line: M = 0.010. Verdicts are those of Tables~\ref{tab:T6},
\ref{tab:T7a}, \ref{tab:T16a} and~\ref{tab:T16b}; a contrast at or above M does not pass by itself.}

The arms that did not pass, each on crack relF1@2 against its comparator at OP-A unless stated:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Skeleton Recall loss} \citep{kirchhoff2024skeletonrecall}: +0.0039 [$-$0.0027, +0.0115], SCREEN FAIL, closed. At
  argmax its recall gain comes with 12\% wider cracks (width ratio 1.13) and lower precision; with the width matched, every crack
  metric is within 0.005 of PF. Our reading (judgement): the loss moves the operating point, not the frontier.
  \item \textbf{SegFormer MiT-B2} \citep{xie2021segformer}: $-$0.0026 [$-$0.0132, +0.0095] at OP-A and $-$0.0135 [$-$0.0259,
  $-$0.0005] at OP-W, SCREEN FAIL, closed. It draws cracks 1.20 times as wide as labelled, outside the width band for every
  source.
  \item \textbf{Rare-class sampling} from DAFormer \citep{hoyer2022daformer}: $-$0.0085 [$-$0.0187, $-$0.0004], screen FAIL. The
  sampler gains on the rare splice class and pays with crack quality, and its false crack pixels rise to 1.68 times PF.
  \item \textbf{Post-hoc weight EMA} \citep{moralesbrotons2024ema} over the PF runs: +0.0018 [$-$0.0047, +0.0081] against the
  same runs without it, null.
  \item \textbf{Jaccard metric loss} \citep{wang2023jml}: the hard-label control is at $-$0.0004 [$-$0.0086, +0.0064], null; the
  soft-band variant against it is +0.0046 [$-$0.0033, +0.0127], SCREEN FAIL, closed, again an operating-point shift (cracks 7\%
  wider).
  \item \textbf{Selective projection decay on the encoder} \citep{tian2024spd}: its mechanism gate failed (the encoder moved
  further from its start than the gate allowed), so the read is descriptive: +0.0007 [$-$0.0272, +0.0130].
  \item \textbf{Decision layers and inactive arms}: train-time logit offsets (mIoU-R13 at argmax against PF at OP-C, $-$0.0022 [$-$0.0112, +0.0060],
  dropped), an auxiliary inactive head with a layered decision (inactive region recall $-$0.0140 [$-$0.0767, +0.0556], parked),
  a per-source crack bias at inference ($+$0.0003 [$-$0.0046, +0.0132] strict crack IoU, closed) and dual-threshold hysteresis
  (+0.0011 [$-$0.0016, +0.0062], closed). A crack bias fitted for F1 (OP-F) is unstable under A/A ($-$0.0118 [$-$0.0357,
  $-$0.0005] crack IoU) and is printed only.
  \item \textbf{Not run}: a domain-randomisation augmentation \citep{ouyang2021gin} was parked before any run.
\end{itemize}
Skeleton Recall was also read on the leave-source-out instrument U1 (CSIR and BMRK held out, 164 cells): +0.0206
[$-$0.0518, +0.0744], a guard read that decides nothing. Our reading (judgement): on these cells, losses that reshape the crack
drawing (Skeleton Recall, soft-band Jaccard) move along the precision and recall trade-off rather than past it, and the arms
aimed at rare classes buy them with crack quality.

\section{Five-seed confirmations on cv5v3}\label{sec:m-confirm}

The two promoted arms and their combination ran as 5-seed rows on cv5v3, each read once under a rule written before the read
(Table~\ref{tab:T6}; Table~\ref{tab:T16b} collects the rows that did not pass). Figure~\ref{fig:F11} shows the false-pixel and width guards at OP-A.

of PF; shaded: allowed range up to 1.5 $\times$ PF. (b) Crack width ratio arm / PF, pooled and on ARTS; shaded: 0.85 to 1.15. Filled
markers: 532 pool cells; open: 416 clean cells. CS and CS\_D4 (*) are selection-biased; HOUSE has no registered rule. OP-W values are

\paragraph{HRNet-W18 U-Net++.} HRNET $-$ PF is +0.0104 [+0.0012, +0.0203] at 532 cells and OP-A, +0.0117 [+0.0019, +0.0220] at
OP-W, +0.0115 [+0.00001, +0.0259] and +0.0126 [+0.0006, +0.0273] on the 416 clean cells: the headline passes at all four values,
and every seed is positive. Two guards fail: false crack pixels at 2.90 to 4.15 times PF at all four values, and the ARTS width
at 1.162 on the 416 cells at OP-W. The verdict is NOT CONFIRMED, reported as a gain with failing guard items. The gain is recall
at equal precision (relR@2 +0.015 at OP-A and +0.023 at OP-W; relP@2 +0.004 and $-$0.001), and strict crack IoU does not move
(+0.0008 [$-$0.0105, +0.0111]). In absolute terms the false-pixel excess is about 1.8 px per crack-free cell; PF's encoder freeze
makes PF unusually clean on these cells (212 px), so the 1.5 times PF bound is tight for any arm without the freeze. Against
HOUSE, HRNET is at +0.0045 [$-$0.0069, +0.0157], a secondary contrast that decides nothing.

\paragraph{Weight averaging.} The SWAD arm keeps the PF recipe, holds the learning rate constant after warm-up and averages
every iterate of steps 10,000 to 20,000 uniformly; its registered readout (\texttt{swad\_bn}) re-estimates the batch-norm
statistics on the training fold, and its declared secondary readout (\texttt{swad}) keeps them. SWADBN $-$ PF is +0.0070
[$-$0.0007, +0.0156] at 532 cells and OP-A; no value reaches M (the 416-cell OP-A value is +0.0098 [+0.0009, +0.0198]), and
the secondary readout does no better (+0.0064 [$-$0.0009, +0.0138]). The verdict is NOT CONFIRMED (no confirmed gain); SWADBN
also fails 5 guard items (the ARTS width, and false crack pixels at 1.60 times PF). Strict crack IoU rises, +0.0180 [+0.0108,
+0.0262], but this is a line-width convention effect, not a better crack map: re-scored on the 1-px centreline labels the gain
is +0.0047 and no longer passes (Section~\ref{sec:m-robust}). On the screen the same arm had +0.0135; at 5 seeds on the
corrected split it is +0.0070. Our reading (judgement): the screen effect shrank by about half, as an arm selected on a 2-seed
screen is expected to.

\paragraph{HRNet + SWAD.} The combination, read with \texttt{swad\_bn} as registered (COMBO), is at +0.0154 [+0.0065, +0.0260]
at 532 cells and OP-A, +0.0158 [+0.0069, +0.0264] at OP-W, and +0.0167 and +0.0166 on the 416 cells, with every seed positive.
It fails 8 guard items: the ARTS width (1.174 to 1.242) and false crack pixels (1.62 to 1.84 times PF). The verdict is NOT
CONFIRMED, reported as a gain with the failing guards named. Its pre-declared secondary contrasts against each part are NO LOSS
(COMBO $-$ HRNET +0.0050 [$-$0.0041, +0.0141]; COMBO $-$ SWADBN +0.0085 [$-$0.0017, +0.0183]). The \texttt{swad} readout of the
same runs (CS) is at +0.0166 [+0.0082, +0.0263] (selection-biased: this readout was later chosen on these cells), keeps the false crack pixels at PF's level and fails only the ARTS width on the
416 cells; because it was declared secondary, it cannot be claimed from this read.

So no trained arm was confirmed on CV5 under its registered rule: HRNet and the combination gain on the headline but fail guards,
and weight averaging does not reach the margin. Our reading (judgement): the failing guards are partly a property of the
reference. PF is unusually clean on crack-free cells, and the width guard is relative to PF, which draws ARTS cracks thinner than they
are labelled (on the 131 ARTS cells among the 416 clean cells, PF's width ratio is 0.7832 and that of CS\_D4 0.9078;
Table~\ref{tab:T7b}). An arm
that draws ARTS cracks closer to the label width then fails the band, so the guard measures an annotation convention as much as a
model.

\section{Test-time augmentation}\label{sec:m-tta}

D4 test-time augmentation predicts each cell in 8 views (four quarter turns, each with and without a flip), maps the softmax of
each view back and averages them. It needs no training and costs 8 times the forward passes. Table~\ref{tab:T7a} gives the
registered TTA read on cv5v3 (5 folds by 5 seeds per arm), and Table~\ref{tab:T7b} its guard values and the earlier read it
replicates.

\paragraph{D4 on PF.} PF\_D4 $-$ PF is +0.0134 [+0.0066, +0.0207] at 532 cells and OP-A, and +0.0132 at each of the other three
values, with no failing guard item: CONFIRMED. False crack pixels on the crack-free cells fall from 212 to 32 (0.15 times PF), and
the pooled width ratio is 1.021. The result replicates an earlier TTA read on the other machine and split (cv5v2, 2 PF seeds),
where 8 views gave +0.0173 [+0.0115, +0.0230] (printed beside) and a 24-view variant with scales passed its registered score claim
at +0.0142 [+0.0079, +0.0202]. This is the one confirmed CV5 crack gain of the thesis, and it holds for cells of known modules: on E1
the D4 part is not resolved (Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result}).

\paragraph{D4 on the candidate.} On the HRNet + SWAD candidate with the \texttt{swad} readout, CS\_D4 $-$ CS is +0.0055
[$-$0.0002, +0.0115] (selection-biased: the \texttt{swad} readout was chosen on these cells): NOT CONFIRMED, NO LOSS. D4 still
halves the candidate's false crack pixels (213 to 98; selection-biased) and fails no guard; with the same views on both sides,
CS\_D4 $-$ PF\_D4 is +0.0087 [+0.0017, +0.0157] (descriptive; selection-biased). Our reading (judgement): D4
and the model change overlap, so on the candidate D4 adds less than on PF. The read raised a flag because D4 costs 8 times the
inference without a confirmed gain on the candidate; we kept D4 in the final recipe before the sealed opening was registered.

\section{The final recipe and its CV5 check}\label{sec:m-final}

With no confirmed single arm, the combination's registered rule had no fallback to name, and we chose the final recipe from the
evidence above: C2 = HRNet-W18 U-Net++ trained under the PF recipe with SWAD weight averaging, the \texttt{swad} readout, and D4.
The \texttt{swad} readout was chosen after the combination read on these same cells, and D4 was adopted from the earlier TTA read.
Both choices used pool cells, so every CS and CS\_D4 number in this section is selection-biased; the unbiased test is the E1
opening of Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result}.

\paragraph{The check.} CS\_D4 $-$ PF is +0.0222 [+0.0138, +0.0308] at 532 cells and OP-A, +0.0228 at OP-W, and +0.0230 and
+0.0229 on the 416 cells (Table~\ref{tab:T7a}, item (c); examples in Figure~\ref{fig:F21}). On CV5 the C2 recipe (CS\_D4) passes its check against PF
(selection-biased); the ARTS width guard fails on the 416 clean cells at OP-A and OP-W (1.159 against the 1.15 band; named) and
passes on the 532 cells. The other guards pass: false crack pixels at 0.46 times PF (98 against 212), mIoU-R13 +0.0117
[+0.0046, +0.0193], and inactive IoU +0.0328 [$-$0.0128, +0.0826], which is within its allowed drop but is not a gain. Strict
crack IoU is +0.0288 [+0.0197, +0.0374], outside the convention band, and decides nothing. Per seed, the plain candidate beats PF
in all 5 seeds (CS $-$ PF +0.014 to +0.022). Here ``C2'' is the CV5 version of the recipe (the cv5v3 fold models); the full-pool
models of Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result} have no CV5 score of their own.

\paragraph{The HOUSE rule.} The protocol prints any in-distribution crack gain also against HOUSE. The two HOUSE-rule rows of
Table~\ref{tab:T7a} were read afterwards, are selection-biased, and decide nothing. CS\_D4 $-$ HOUSE, the rule as written, is
+0.0163 [+0.0051, +0.0284] at 532 cells and OP-A, +0.0194 [+0.0080, +0.0319] at OP-W, and +0.0164 [+0.0033, +0.0318] and
+0.0204 [+0.0073, +0.0369] on the 416 cells; strict crack IoU is +0.0110 [+0.0029, +0.0198], inside the band. Two items are
outside a bound with HOUSE as reference: the ARTS width at OP-A, 0.847 (532) and 0.846 (416), just below 0.85, because HOUSE
itself draws wide ARTS cracks. As written, the rule compares an 8-view arm with a single-view HOUSE, and no HOUSE D4 predictions
exist. The single-view line, CS $-$ HOUSE, is +0.0107 [$-$0.0009, +0.0229] at OP-A, holding 0 at OP-A on both cell sets, and
+0.0140 [+0.0017, +0.0269] at OP-W. Our reading (judgement): in distribution, the HRNet + SWAD model alone is not separated
from HOUSE at argmax, and D4 supplies the rest of the edge.

\paragraph{The crack target.} The operating target set at the start of this work, relR@2 of at least 0.90 at relP@2 of at least
0.90, is not reached on CV5 either: at OP-A, CS\_D4 is at relP@2 0.9216 and relR@2 0.8211 (selection-biased), and PF at 0.8953
and 0.8024 (Figure~\ref{fig:F12}).

\section{Robustness of the contrasts}\label{sec:m-robust}

The dataset audit (Chapter~\ref{ch:data}) re-scored the same out-of-fold predictions of the 5-seed rows on four label variants
(the older release, the 1-px centreline of v1.1, a 3-px redraw, and v1.1 with the audit's label-error candidates ignored) and
removed module groups greedily until each relF1@2 contrast reached 0 (Table~\ref{tab:T19b}). The relF1@2 contrasts survive every
variant, shifting by at most 0.003: COMBO $-$ PF stays a pass under all four (+0.0128 to +0.0161), and HRNET $-$ PF moves from
+0.0104 to +0.0073 on the 3-px labels, below M. The strict-IoU gains of HOUSE and SWAD do not survive a change of line-width
convention: on the centreline labels they fall to +0.0015 and +0.0047. The crack contrasts that pass rest on few module groups:
the HRNet + SWAD contrast reaches 0 after removing 31 groups, the HRNet contrast after 17, and HOUSE's relF1@2 edge after 4, out of
the 125 groups that hold crack pixels. Greedy removal is a worst case, not a sampling distribution. Our reading (judgement):
relF1@2 is the right decision metric for these labels, and a strict-IoU gain on this data should be read as a statement about the
width of the drawn line until shown otherwise.

\section{Weak classes}\label{sec:m-weak}

The goal of this work (Chapter~\ref{ch:intro}) names the other classes as well as crack and inactive. On the pool, gridline, splice, edge dark and material are
the weak ones, and no training arm here was aimed at them except rare-class sampling, which failed
(Section~\ref{sec:m-screens}). One decision layer was tested: OP-C adds per-class biases for 11 of the 13 robust classes
(background and crack stay at 0), fitted on the other 4 folds. It passed its pre-set rule on both machines, raising mIoU-R13 by
+0.0126 [+0.0077, +0.0167] on the machine of the 5-seed rows and by +0.0137 [+0.0107, +0.0169] on the other, mostly through
gridline (+0.058 and +0.060) and splice (+0.075 and +0.092); about a third of the gain is splice at a precision of 0.15 to 0.20.
OP-C was adopted as the comparator for arms that shift class priors, an entry of the decision-layer class, but no final model
uses it. On E1 material and edge dark stay at 0 for every arm (Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result}). The weak classes are not solved in
this work.

\section{Summary}\label{sec:m-summary}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Under equal training, two models of the EL papers sit below PF on crack relF1@2 (UCF-EL's DeepLabV3 $-$0.0773, Pratt's
  weighted loss $-$0.0932), and a third (Hanifi's VGG-16 U-Net) is not separated from it; no decoder effect was detected, an output-stride effect was (2-seed screens on cv5v2).
  \item None of the 5-seed rows was confirmed: HRNet-W18 and HRNet + SWAD gain on the headline but fail
  guards, and weight averaging does not reach the margin; its strict-IoU gain is a line-width convention effect.
  \item D4 TTA on PF is CONFIRMED on CV5 (+0.0134 [+0.0066, +0.0207]); on the candidate it is NOT CONFIRMED, NO LOSS.
  \item The final recipe C2 (HRNet-W18 U-Net++ + SWAD, \texttt{swad} readout, + D4) passes its CV5 check against PF,
  selection-biased, with the ARTS width guard failing on the 416 clean cells (named). The unbiased test is E1
  (Chapter~\ref{ch:e1_result}).
  \item The 0.90 / 0.90 crack target is not reached, and the weak classes are not solved.
\end{itemize}
