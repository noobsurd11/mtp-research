# Related work: published pixel-level EL segmentation numbers, and why they cannot be compared

related_work, 22 Nov 2026, for the lead (gap G-N10; PROTOCOL v0.2 Open 9 and §6.6 item 4). Written from papers and team files only: no GPU job, no server, no sealed label or prediction read.

**Tags.** E = stated in the cited source (paper, or a team file for our own measurements). S = judgement of this file. "inferred" = a convention not stated by the paper, with what it was inferred from. "PDF p." = page of the PDF file read; journal page numbers are given where printed. A number is never computed here; arithmetic checks are marked as such.

**Sources.** Local full texts (md5 in `RW_SOURCES.tsv`): `/home/yugma/papers/`, `~/mtp/research/papers/`, `~/mtp/research/literature/pdfs_inst/`, `/home/noob/work/docs/papers/`. Open-access PDFs fetched today (SIGAN, Mayr, Yi, Hijjawi) stay in the session tool-results folder. Team files: `harness/final/PUBLIC_BENCH.md` (public_scout, 19 Nov), `results/final_opening_read/verdict.txt` l88 to l94, `thesis/THESIS_TABLES.md` T13a, handbook doc 05, literature notes A01 to A10, F04, F06, paper cards `cards/in2/`. The Research MCP was not available in this session (authentication only), so discovery used the ledgers, notes, local libraries and web search.

**Scope.** Pixel-level segmentation (or pixel-level anomaly maps) of EL images of solar cells, 2018 to 2026. Image-level and box-only work is in table 3. Rows R are read in full text (22 papers); rows A are abstract-level or second-hand (7 papers) and are not counted in section 4.

**Our protocol, for the last column** (PROTOCOL_v0.2 §0): BenchmarkEL `dataset_20211104`, label set v1.1, CV5-G module-grouped folds, sealed test50 and E1 (unseen modules); headline crack relF1@2 (tolerance 2 px) with relP@2 / relR@2, strict crack IoU, inactive IoU, mIoU-R13; operating point OP-A (argmax); 5 seeds per table row, group-bootstrap intervals. "yes" would need the same cells, labels, metric and a seed count; "partly" means the published metric can be recomputed on our per-cell records for the same cells (T13b), but not our card; "no" means another test set or an incompatible metric.

Readings (S unless marked):
1. **One data family, many test sets.** Only three papers score on test50 (Pratt 2023, SEiPV-Net, PV-S3 CSB-S1, the last with its split unstated). Torpey uses release `20220723`; Mazen, Ramadan and Hanifi use `20221008` (Hanifi re-split it and did not release the split). Mazen Tables 1 to 3, Ramadan Table 11 and PV-S3 Table 6 still rank their numbers against test50 rows (E). The same holds for UCF-EL (official release, own 80/10/10 splits, and the unreleased paper data) and PVEL-S.
2. **No published split is module-disjoint, and where we could check, test overlaps training.** test50 has 8 physical-cell duplicates of pool cells (E, T13a); PV-S3's released lists put 1,769 of 2,289 UCF-EL test images in train lists (E, handbook 05 §4.2); Fioresi validates on the test set each epoch (E, PDF p. 5); Duan evaluates on its validation set (E); MultiSolSegment and Saleh describe augmenting before the split (E wording, S consequence).
3. **Almost no uncertainty.** 18 of 22 papers report no interval, seed count or repeat at all; the four others give intervals on a side quantity (Torpey: Tukey CIs for crack and five runs per regime, with R7 and R8 run once; PV-S3: SD over three samplings at 20% labels; Ramadan: 3-fold CV on training data; MultiSolSegment: CV accuracy). None gives an interval on the headline test number.
4. **Convention moves the number as much as the method.** Same models, same 50 cells, our own test50 read on v0 labels (E, `verdict.txt` l100 to l116, C1, mean over seeds): Pratt mean of 3 defect medians 0.405 under rule A, 0.202 under rule B, 0.541 under rule C; SEiPV-style per-image mIoU 0.855 with absent-and-unpredicted = 1 and 0.545 with absent skipped; pooled micro IoU 0.891. Across papers on test50 (PV-S3 split assumed): crack 0.25 as Pratt's per-image median and 0.58 as PV-S3's pooled IoU (E). SEiPV-Net's "IoU" 0.7447 is a pooled pixel quantity with background (IoU = Dice / (2 - Dice) of its Dice 0.8531 within 0.001, E arithmetic); its headline "mIoU" 0.8604 has no stated averaging, and Ramadan prints it as wIoU (E). On PVEL-S, STC-Net prints MIoU 95.98 on "validation" and 72.52 on the 360 test images (E, Tables I, II): the 0.95 to 0.97 PVEL-S numbers of Duan, Ramadan and STC-Net are validation-set numbers with background in a binary mean.
5. **Nothing published measures what our protocol measures.** No paper uses a distance tolerance for the 1 to 2 px crack labels, only Torpey repeats training runs behind its headline table, none reports on unseen modules. So no published row is comparable with relF1@2, OP-A or E1; three rows (R02, R03, R06a) are "partly" comparable on test50 by re-scoring our models in their convention (T13b), as context only.
