## D. New ideas to validate (ranked by cost, then expected value)
For each idea, check the premise in the data and look for prior tries in the repo (avoid repeats), then check the literature and the cost. Propose a protocol or give a reason to drop it.

1. **E1 threshold / calibration check (CPU, ~1 h).**
   - Plot E1 crack recall and precision against the threshold for FINAL and SUP100-PF.
   - If E1 cracks show up at lower thresholds, it's calibration, not features: test per-panel threshold adaptation (e.g. a per-module quantile).
   - This tells us what kind of problem E1 is. Diagnostic only; E1 is never for selection.
2. **Per-module intensity normalisation (cheap).**
   - EL brightness depends on injection current, which differs per panel.
   - Normalise each cell by its module's statistics, or use CLAHE / local contrast normalisation.
   - Check first: intensity stats of E1 vs train cells.
3. **Neighbour-reference channel (label-free, aimed at unseen panels).**
   - For each cell, build a "healthy reference" from the median of the other cells in the same module (aligned, same type).
   - Feed cell minus reference as an extra input channel, so defects stand out against their own panel's normal.
   - Check: module images / cell grid available for train, test and E1; alignment of half-cut vs full cells.
4. **Periodic-structure removal channel.**
   - Busbars and fingers are periodic; cracks are not.
   - Remove the grid pattern with a Fourier notch filter (or a learned one) and feed the residual as a second channel.
   - Should also help scuff (texture).
5. **Synthetic half-cut cells from labelled full cells.**
   - Our 582 have 0 half-cuts, and 75% of E1's failure is half-cut + scuff.
   - Simulate: cut labelled full cells, add the dark cut line and spacing, rescale/flip, and keep the crack/inactive labels. Optionally add scuff-like texture.
   - Before training, compare against real half-cut appearance (Pratt/Hanifi half-cuts, the pool's 986).
   - Validate on the Hanifi half-cells (non-E1); report E1 as descriptive.
6. **Higher input resolution / HR branch.**
   - Cracks are 2-3 px, and the encoder downsamples heavily.
   - Train at 2x, or add a high-resolution skip path. Check the current input size and memory.
7. **Industry-style metrics (no GPU).**
   - Cell-level crack detection (precision/recall/AUROC) and inactive-area-% error, next to pixel IoU.
   - The pixel ceiling is ~0.5 (labelling noise), and cell-level numbers may show real usefulness. Strong for the paper.
8. **Foundation encoder done properly.**
   - DINOv3 (arXiv 2508.10104) or a SAM/SAM2 encoder, with a PF-style or LoRA fine-tune, plus a high-resolution decoder or CNN branch (16-px patches vs 2-3 px cracks).
   - Start with a cheap probe (Track D D1 design).
   - Motivation: PF says preserved general features help unseen panels, and this is the stronger version.
9. **Test-time adaptation (label-free).**
   - Adapt BN statistics / use entropy minimisation per new panel at inference (e.g. TENT-style).
   - Check whether BN-HC already covers part of this.
10. **AI-assisted labelling (SAM-based).**
    - Regions (inactive, scuff): SAM masks, near-automatic.
    - Cracks: model + SAM proposals, with a human correcting them (faster than drawing).
    - Use on the 986 pool half-cells and to clean the 24 Hanifi half-cut crack cells.
    - Warning: Hanifi's SAM crack labels are the noisy ones (specks, 1-px lines), so cracks need human correction.
    - Related: Boxes2Pixels (arXiv 2604.11162), training on noisy SAM masks.
11. **Appearance augmentation for unseen panels.**
    - Strong brightness/contrast/gamma, blur, noise, busbar-count variation, and synthetic cut lines (with idea 5).
    - Check what the house augmentation already does.
