## 1. Question
Does training on synthetic half-cut cells, made from labelled full cells, make SUP100-PF more robust on half-cut cells (E1's unseen half-cut modules), without a test cost?
- **Contrast:** HC − SUP100-PF, 5 v 5, seeds 5-9.

## 2. Why this is not a repeat of R4 (judge condition 3)
- R4 (Track M) added 43 real Hanifi half-cuts and E1 half-cut recall did not move (0.133 → 0.123).
- Likely reason (E + S): those cells' crack GT is 1,792 px of edge marks and specks over 17 cells, 48% within 10 px of spacing, border or the cut line (judge). So R4 effectively taught "half-cuts have almost no crack", which may have reinforced the measured under-confidence on half-cuts.
- HC transfers clean full-cell crack and inactive labels into half-cut geometry: the opposite signal.
