# Why standard SOTA SSL does not work on our EL crack, and what to do next

C (critic and moderator), 24 Oct 2026, after two challenge rounds with R1 (data side) and R2 (method side). Both converged; open items in §3.
Trail: `critique.md` (every claim, verdict and C's recomputes), `r1_data_side.md`, `r2_method_side.md`.
All numbers: cv02, split seed 0, 50 test cells, revised masks, final-step weights, unless stated. **E** measured, **R** reading, **S** speculation. No GPU was used; all new numbers are CPU recomputes of existing outputs.

## 1. The answer

**Established (E).** First a correction to the question: SSL is not doing nothing on crack. BsM5 beats supervised on crack IoU at 5% (+0.029, passes), and with the free logit bias on both sides it still passes (+0.037, thr 0.013; +0.035 without twins and the noisy cell; C's recompute of `results/sk1/la_sa_test.json`). The "only up to a free bias" reading holds only when the bias is given to supervised alone. What is true: the gain is small (a third of what +24 random labelled cells buy, +0.092) and it is bound to what the labelled draw already teaches. Without twins it is +0.047 on CFVS (thr 0.012), +0.002 on BMRK and −0.030 on ARTS; BMRK and ARTS hold 30% of test crack and have 2 and 0 labelled crack cells. The model's crack belief is bimodal: 57% of GT crack scores below 0.001 and 35% at or above 0.95, so a rule applied to the final model can act on 8% (A5) to 19% (BsM5) of GT crack (`results/al1/hist_revised.npz`). About 38% of pool crack is a true miss more than 2 px from any prediction; every training-time rule we ran moved that by at most 0.046 (`skcdf/second_opinion_a2.md`). Inactive is the counter-case under equal scarcity (pool/labelled px 11.5 vs 12.1 for crack): its supervised error is under-extended regions (precision 0.77, recall 0.28), which self-training extends (recall 0.43), so SSL gives +0.10 to +0.13 and survives the LA yardstick. The two methods that failed outright failed for measured, method-specific reasons: ABSH corrects a prior collapse we do not have (SK1 crack CI [−0.010, +0.002]); ALHVR gives no loss to the region holding 65-70% of GT crack, and its DTCT loss halves inactive recall (AL1 WORSE on all three).
**Likely (R).** Self-training spreads labels through neighbourhoods of what the teacher already gets right (the expansion assumption, Wei et al. `arxiv:2010.03622`). ARTS has nothing to spread from; faint BMRK cracks overlap background; inactive regions look alike across sources and spread well. Published gains come mostly from weak or from-scratch baselines with classes at 0 Dice (Synapse, DINO-Mix `arxiv:2602.07819` Table 1 per R2) or from blob-like classes; our laddered, pretrained, thresholded, separate-BN base had already taken the cheap part. Oliver et al. (`arxiv:1804.09170`) name the two conditions we fail: labelled set i.i.d. with the pool (ours differs by source) and a val set large enough to tune (ours has 6 inactive cells).
**Speculative (S).** That untried methods (adaptive thresholds, UniMatch feature perturbation, copy-paste, foundation teachers, MIM) would fail too; that tuning would add no more than ~0.01; that BMRK's low score is intrinsic rather than label artefact. None was run.
**Not a cause:** pool size (P9 28× pool adds nothing; 119M px vs ACDC 77M); the noisy cell and the twins (they distort measurement, not mechanism; twin removal from the pool leaves the non-twin gain at +0.0197); strict-IoU compression (it hides part of SSL's gain, 17% of the gap to 100% labels vs 29% on relF1@2, but changes no verdict).

**Not proposed:** class-adaptive thresholds (final-model band is 8-19% of GT crack, more negatives than positives in it); routing or prototype variants (AL1); balanced heads (SK1); thin-structure losses (P14 null; misses are faint, not broken); the DTCT-entropy one-change arm (explains AL1's inactive harm in a dropped method; not worth GPU); R1's source-matched pool (D5: informative but ~14 GPU-h for a claim the per-source table already makes descriptively).

## 3. Open disagreements
1. **D1's primary contrast.** R2: source-targeted vs random (T − R), cheaper. R1: R / Tc / Ts with Ts − Tc primary, because targeting adds 12 crack cells and random adds ~5, so T − R confounds source with crack count. C sides with R1 on design, the user decides on cost and on the seed-rule exception.
2. **BMRK: hard or mislabelled?** R1: partly intrinsic (blurriest, 4-px labels, downscaled ELPV, 38% label placement within 1 px). Neither can settle it; the audit (17 of 40 components are BMRK) can.
3. **Can a training-time rule reach true misses?** R2: possible in principle (BsM5 moved 12 points out of bin 0), unshown in practice (≤ 0.046 on the pool). C: nothing we ran did; not worth a run without a new mechanism.
4. **Untuned recipe.** A tuned BsM could hide ~0.01 crack IoU (R2, conceded). Not separable with a 54-cell val; stays a stated limit.

## 4. What the thesis can already claim
1. At 5% labels, hard-PL Mean Teacher with separate BN and CutMix beats a 5-seed step-matched supervised baseline on all three primaries (BsM5, secondary in P13, re-run on the twin-free pool: +0.027 / +0.132 / +0.041).
2. On crack the gain is small and equals a free logit bias in argmax size; with the bias on both sides it still passes (+0.037; +0.035 without twins and noisy cell). Post hoc, τ fixed after SK1, BsM5+LA loses 0.08 crack precision: report as such.
3. The crack gain is source-bound: CFVS +0.047, BMRK +0.002, ARTS −0.030 (no twins, descriptive). +24 random labels buy ~3× more crack IoU than SSL.
4. On inactive, SSL buys more than doubling the labels (+0.107 vs +0.041 for 5% → 10%), survives LA, and transfers to unseen modules (cell IoU +0.47 [+0.32, +0.59], CsM5 on the 106-cell extension). Few-cell caveat every time.
5. Crack scores are bimodal; about 38% of pool crack is a true miss that no pseudo-label rule we ran reaches (≤ 12% recovered).
6. Negative results with mechanisms: ALHVR-EL is WORSE than supervised (routing leaves 65-70% of rare pixels unlossed; DTCT halves inactive recall, a reading); SKCDF's balanced head is an informative null (crack CI excludes gains above +0.002) because there is no prior collapse to fix; P17 decoupled decoder n.d.
7. The label version (+0.082, P14) and the supervised recipe (P6) are bigger levers than any SSL loss measured.

Limits on all of the above: one split, 50 test cells, 2 labelled inactive cells at 5%, untuned SSL, cv02 only.
