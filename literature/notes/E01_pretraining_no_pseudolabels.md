# Using unlabelled data without pseudo-labels
- **Torpey et al. 2024** (see A02): in-domain SimCLR on 22k EL images gives 0.517 against random init 0.486, ImageNet-SimCLR 0.534 and COCO 0.544 (DeepLabV3 R50, full labels). **In-domain contrastive pretraining underperformed out-of-domain pretraining at full labels.** The authors suggest SSL objectives may not capture the downstream-relevant information under a large shift. Label-scarce regimes were not tested.
- **MAE** (He et al., CVPR 2022, arXiv 2111.06377) and **DINOv2** (Oquab et al., arXiv 2304.07193). Generic SSL. Read: titles.
- **AOI-SSL** (Figueira et al., arXiv 2605.12430, May 2026): small-domain self-supervised pretraining of ViTs for semiconductor optical-inspection segmentation. MAE was most effective in the small-data setting and beat scratch and ImageNet under a fixed fine-tuning budget. Read: search abstract.
  - Relevance: the closest industrial analogue for MAE on 12k–150k EL cells. Masked reconstruction might fit EL (periodic busbar and finger structure) better than SimCLR's augmentation invariances, which can erase thin cracks (blur, crop). [reader-inferred hypothesis]
- **Models Genesis** (Zhou et al., arXiv 1908.06912) and **Azizi et al. 2021** (arXiv 2101.05224). Domain-specific self-supervision beats ImageNet transfer, especially with few labels (medical). Read: abstracts.
- Transfer from neighbouring labelled EL data: UCF-EL (crack labels, pixel-level), PVEL-AD (boxes), ELPV (cell labels). No paper was found that pretrains on UCF-EL and fine-tunes on BenchmarkEL. [bounded: searches in this pass]
