# Lei et al. 2025: Adaptive Learning of High-Value Regions for Semi-Supervised Medical Image Segmentation (ALHVR)
- ICCV 2025. https://mlanthology.org/iccv/2025/lei2025iccv-adaptive · supp https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/supplemental/Lei_Adaptive_Learning_of_ICCV_2025_supplemental.pdf · code https://github.com/ziziyao/ALHVR
- Read: full (local PDF) + reference code (see analysis/alhvr_audit.md, papers_metrics.md).
- Problem: SSL methods suppress low-confidence pixels. ALHVR asks which regions carry the most learning value.
- Method:
  - Two networks (CPS-style init). Pixels are split by a batch-adaptive confidence threshold γ into Ω1 (both confident; no loss), Ω2 (one confident) and Ω3 (neither confident).
  - Ω2 → CG-CPCL: cross-network prototype consistency.
  - Ω3 → DTCT: per pixel, the more confident network's clean prediction, sharpened, teaches the other's perturbed output. This descends from MCF's dynamic competitive pseudo-labels.
- Evidence for the premise (Table 1): on ACDC 10% with CPS, masking out the reliable *stable* region during training gives the highest Dice. So the "high-value" regions are the unstable or unreliable ones.
- Data: ACDC (4 classes, U-Net), AbdomenCT-1K, BraTS.
- Recipe:
  - SGD lr 0.01, momentum 0.9, wd 1e-4, 30k iterations.
  - λ = 0.1·sigmoid ramp over the whole run.
  - Best-val checkpoint every 200 iterations.
- Weaknesses (empirical; read: full):
  - Designed and validated on few-class, large-structure medical data. No class-imbalance or rare-class analysis (sections checked: §1, §3, §4). [bounded absence]
  - DTCT's elementwise sharpening (T = 0.1) gives near-zero targets when the winner's top probability is below 0.5. This is benign with 4 classes and harmful with 24 [reader-inferred; measured by our audit].
  - Best-val selection on 10 validation patients. [author code]
- Relevance: the inspiration. **Our measurements:**
  - Ω3 holds 71% of true crack pixels.
  - The winner's crack accuracy in Ω3 is about 62%.
  - Cross-network prototypes reach 4.6% crack accuracy (1.1% per-batch).
  - So the premise ("value is in ambiguous regions") holds for cracks, but the teaching signal ALHVR uses there is weakest exactly for the rare class.
