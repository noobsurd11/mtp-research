## 2. Detail

### P11, 10% labels (cv02, 3 seeds; `results/p11/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dm − A (pipeline check) | −0.007 | +0.000 | −0.005 |
| Cm − Dm (loss helps) | +0.009 | **+0.071** | **+0.018** |
| Cm − A (beats supervised) | +0.002 | **+0.071** | **+0.013** |
- Bootstrap: Cm − Dm inactive [+0.020, +0.121], survives top-3 removal. Cm − A inactive [−0.009, +0.158], +0.028 without top 3. Cm − A relF1@2 [+0.005, +0.024].
- External pool (Cmx): no loss effect.
- Gap to 100% labels closed by Cm: crack IoU 3%, inactive 53%, relF1@2 21%.

### P12, 5% labels (cv02 3 seeds; gaint replication, seeds 3–5; `results/p12/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dm5 − A5c (pipeline check) | −0.017 | −0.015 | −0.025 |
| Cm5 − Dm5 (loss helps) | **+0.023** | +0.018 | **+0.040** |
| Cm5 − A5c (beats supervised) | +0.006 | +0.003 | +0.015 |
| gCm5 − gDm5 (gaint) | **+0.047** | +0.077 | **+0.078** |
| gCm5 − A5 (gaint) | **+0.029** | +0.068 | **+0.058** |
- The loss's crack gain about cancels the loss-off handicap (Dm5 − A5c crack −0.017 [−0.024, −0.010]). The handicap is consistent with BN mixing; P13 tests that.
- gaint passes the SSL-over-supervised arithmetic (robust over cells: crack IoU [+0.015, +0.040], inactive [+0.022, +0.130]); cv02 does not. Locked rules give gaint no say. The machines disagree with 3 seeds each.
- Inactive is unmeasurable at 5% (seed sd up to 0.14; 2 of 23 labelled cells have it).

### E1, task metrics and the unseen-module extension (`results/e1/README.md`)
- 106 extra BenchmarkEL cells from 67 modules we never trained on (1 shared with our 582; 1 with the external pool).
- Every model loses most crack performance there: L3 100% crack IoU 0.471 → 0.152.
- Tolerant pseudo-labels (P11, P12): on unseen modules, crack false alarms up by ~0.10 of crack-free cells; inactive cell IoU up (+0.07 to +0.21).

### P13, separate BN + CutMix, 5% labels (cv02; A5c and CsM5 5 seeds, others 3; `results/p13/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CsM5 − A5c (primary)** | +0.014 | **+0.117** | **+0.033** |
| BsM5 − A5c (hard PL + CutMix) | **+0.036** | **+0.113** | **+0.052** |
| Cs5 − A5c (no CutMix) | +0.004 | **+0.105** | +0.018 |
| CsM5 − Cs5 (CutMix) | +0.010 | +0.012 | +0.015 |
| CsM5 − BsM5 (tolerant vs hard) | **−0.022** (worse) | +0.004 | −0.019 |
- Primary over cells: inactive [+0.042, +0.189] (+0.103 without top 3); relF1@2 [+0.006, +0.055] (+0.020 without top 3). Every SSL seed beats every supervised seed on test inactive.
- Crack: tolerant PL alone adds ~0 against the clean control (+0.004); P12's +0.023 loss effect mostly offset the joint-BN handicap. CutMix adds ~+0.010 (robust over cells, n.d. on seeds). Hard PL + CutMix gives +0.036 (robust, 3 seeds).
- Unseen modules: CsM5 inactive cell IoU 0.033 → 0.500; crack unchanged. Separate BN cuts the joint-BN false-alarm rise (Cs5 − Cm5 −0.095).
- Val inactive goes the other way (A5c 0.106, CsM5 0.081). Val has 6 inactive cells, 3 hold 89% of its pixels. P16's CutMix pick used val as locked: CutMix **off**.
- No collapse (rare_frac 0.006–0.011, stable or rising). No gaint replication (dropped for P15).

### P14, tolerant supervised loss, 10% labels (cv02, 3 seeds; `results/p14/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| L3 − L3o (label-noise gap) | **+0.082** | −0.015 | **+0.081** |
| L3To − L3o (recovery) | −0.004 | +0.010 | −0.005 |
| L3T − L3 (tolerance on revised) | +0.003 | **+0.037** | +0.002 |
- Gap: [+0.046, +0.129], +0.100 without top 3, both source groups, and +0.030 [+0.012, +0.048] on unseen modules. The revised-label model also wins when scored on the old labels (crack IoU 0.351 vs 0.332).
- Tolerance lowers crack AP (−0.020 to −0.024) and per-cell crack tolF1 on old labels (−0.041 [−0.066, −0.020]).
- L3T inactive gain: [−0.016, +0.093]; +0.028 without top 3. On unseen modules inactive cell IoU +0.088 [+0.005, +0.193], crack false alarms +0.041 (n.d.).

### P15, UCF-EL encoder transfer (gaint, 3 seeds; `results/p15/README.md`)

|---|---|---|---|
| gU5 − A5 (5%) | +0.0096 | −0.012 | +0.015 |

### P16, SSL at 20/50/100% labels (cv02, 5 vs 5 seeds; `results/p16/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 | Without `CSIR_00070_r8_c4` (post hoc): crack / inactive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S20 − A20 | −0.040 | +0.029 | −0.070 | +0.002 / +0.029 |
| S50 − A50 | +0.039 | +0.006 | +0.053 | +0.002 / +0.002 |
| Sx100 − A100 (external pool) | +0.028 | +0.003 | +0.040 | −0.002 / +0.002 |
- All n.d. under the locked rule, with and without the cell. Thresholds are large (0.069–0.091 on crack) because of the cell.
- CutMix off: P13 val scores were CsM5 0.351, Cs5 0.365. So P16 tests Cs (tolerant PL, separate BN), not P13's passing CsM recipe.
- **The noisy cell.** In S20 s2/s4, A50 s2 and A100 s3, the model calls 15–31% of the cell crack (39k–80k false-positive px). That cuts the run's crack IoU by 0.11–0.18.
  - The cell is the noisiest of all 582 (noise sigma 46 vs median 1.5).
  - It hit 2 supervised and 2 SSL runs, and val doesn't show it.
  - It also caused P6's `L0_10pct_s1` outlier, which makes up most of P6's secondary "L1 − L0 crack IoU +0.048" (about +0.010 without it).
- **Inactive gain shrinks with labels:** +0.105 at 5% (P13 Cs5), then +0.029, +0.006, +0.003.
- **Unseen modules (secondary):**
  - S20 and S50 raise inactive cell IoU (+0.18, +0.20; CIs above 0);
  - S50 crack IoU +0.019 [+0.007, +0.031];
  - S20 crack false alarms +0.046 (worse);
  - Sx100 n.d. on everything.
- The unlabelled loss is 2–7% of the supervised loss at 20–100% (46% at 5%). λ is fixed at 1, so the SSL term carries little weight there (reading, untested).

### P13 Amendment 2 and P17 (13 Oct; `results/p17/README.md`, `results/p13/README.md`)
| Contrast (5 vs 5) | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BsM5 − A5c | **+0.029** | **+0.107** | **+0.045** | passes (secondary) |
| CsM5 − BsM5 | −0.015 | +0.010 | −0.012 | n.d. |
| **BsD5 − BsM5 (P17 primary)** | −0.006 | +0.011 | −0.008 | **n.d.** |
| BsD5 − A5c | **+0.023** | **+0.117** | **+0.037** | passes |
- BsM5 seeds 3–4 came in lower on crack (0.317, 0.327 vs 0.335–0.343). Its sd rose from 0.004 to 0.011, which is why rule 5 flipped.
- P17 keeps pseudo-labels out of the labelled decoder: a second decoder learns from them and tracks the first by EMA (SKCDF Eq. 6). Its built-in check passed: logs equal BsM5's to step 2,000.
- Reading: most of BsM5's gain reaches the teacher without the decoder fitting pseudo-labels. A share below about 40% is not excluded. The decoupled teacher ranks pixels worse (crack AP 0.310 vs 0.367).
- Unseen modules, BsD5 − BsM5: crack tolF1 +0.016 [+0.002, +0.033], false alarms +0.049 [+0.011, +0.089], inactive area error −0.065 pp.

### P18, BsM recipe at 10% and 20% (13 Oct, EXPLORATORY; `results/p18/README.md`)
The recipe was chosen after seeing P13's test results, so this is arithmetic, not a verdict. cv02, BsM20 5 seeds, BsM10 3 seeds; the pipeline check passed.

| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| BsM10 − A10 (3 v 3) | +0.012 | **+0.132** | +0.013 |
| BsM20 − A20 (5 v 5) | −0.006 | **+0.060** | −0.018 |
| same, without `CSIR_00070_r8_c4` (post hoc): BsM10 − A10 | **+0.017** | **+0.138** | **+0.020** |
| same, without that cell: BsM20 − A20 | +0.013 (thr 0.014) | **+0.061** | +0.012 (thr 0.013) |
| BsM20 − S20 (two changes), without that cell | +0.010 | +0.032 | **+0.011** |
- BsM20 s4 puts 34,265 crack FP px on the noisy cell (crack IoU 0.320, others 0.397–0.422). Supervised seeds fail there too from 50% up (A50 s2, A100 s3).
- BsM10's test inactive IoU 0.426 is about supervised 100%'s 0.429. Test inactive is concentrated (top-3 cells hold 59% of GT px), and val inactive disagrees at 20% (BsM20 0.374 vs A20 0.452).
- Unseen modules (E1 ext): BsM − supervised inactive detection F1 +0.41 (10%) and +0.51 (20%), crack tolF1 +0.05 / +0.07, with more false crack cells (+0.06).
- So P16's "no gain from 20% up" was at least partly the Cs recipe's (S20 − A20 without the cell: +0.002 / +0.029 / +0.000).

### E-SCDD audit (12 Oct; `results/escdd_audit/README.md`, handbook doc 17)
- E-SCDD's crack and inactive masks are pixel-identical to BenchmarkEL's own `20221008` release. The P3/P14 label effect is BenchmarkEL's correction, which E-SCDD redistributes.
- The release has 903 labelled images (693 + 117 new real + 93 synthetic), not the paper's 1,000. 100 of the 117 new cells are already in our external unlabelled pool.
