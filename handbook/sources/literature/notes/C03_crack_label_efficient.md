# Label-efficient crack segmentation outside PV
- **König et al. 2021** (arXiv 2109.00456): weakly supervised surface-crack segmentation from classifier localisation plus thresholding; "comparable performance to fully supervised" on four crack datasets. Read: abstract.
- **Crack-Segmenter** (Kyem et al., arXiv 2510.10378, Oct 2025): self-supervised multi-scale transformer for pavement cracks, annotation-free, claims to beat 13 supervised methods on 10 datasets. Read: abstract. Weaknesses: not assessed. The claim is strong enough to need the full text before citing.
