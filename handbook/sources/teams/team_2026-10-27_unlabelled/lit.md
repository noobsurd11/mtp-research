## 1. Torpey et al. arxiv:2402.17611, checked [full: §3-5]
- 642 labelled BenchmarkEL-family cells, 28 classes. **Full labels only: no label-fraction curve.**
- 22k unlabelled released (64k built). R6 EL-SimCLR used the full set; CCT / U2PL a 5k subset.
- DeepLabV3-R50; best-on-val checkpoint; **CCT and U2PL run once**.
- mIoU: random 0.486; ImageNet-sup 0.508; IN-SimCLR 0.534; IN-MoCov2 0.508; **EL-SimCLR 0.517**; COCO-seg 0.544; **CCT 0.550**; **U2PL 0.448, below random init**; CCT encoder re-fine-tuned 0.508.
- COCO, IN-SimCLR and CCT are statistically equivalent on mIoU.
- **Statistics (judge round 1):** Tukey HSD. For crack the unit is the run: image-level IoU is block-centred (each image's mean over all runs subtracted), then averaged per regime, giving n = 5 runs per regime and n = 1 for CCT and U2PL (§4.4). Run-to-run sd is not given in text; mIoU differences among the top three are "not statistically significant when comparing the average mIoU across runs". So EL-SimCLR vs IN-sup (+0.009) is very likely n.s. too (not tested in text).
- **Crack IoU: COCO (R2) and IN-SimCLR (R4) beat every other regime, CCT included, p ≤ 0.0001.** No absolute crack numbers in text (figure only). With n = 1 for CCT, "beats CCT" rests on one CCT run.
- **Correction to earlier team text:** EL-SimCLR did not lose to ImageNet *supervised* on mIoU (0.517 vs 0.508). It lost to ImageNet *SimCLR* (0.534) and COCO (0.544), and lost on crack.
- Authors' own control: same SimCLR on Cityscapes in-domain 0.189 vs OOD 0.243. Their reading: crop-based multi-view objectives suit object-centric data; two crops of a cell hold different defects.
- **What it shows for us:** on our data family, at full labels, (a) a different pretraining source (COCO segmentation) was the biggest lever; (b) in-domain contrastive was +0.009 mIoU over ImageNet-sup and lost on crack; (c) one SSL method helped mIoU in a single run but not crack, another was worse than random. It says nothing about MIM or about scarce labels.

## 3. Theory of when unlabelled data helps (R)
- Self-training works when each class's region expands into its neighbourhood under augmentation (expansion), so the teacher's correct region grows (arxiv:2010.03622).
- Unlabelled data helps only when the decision boundary lies where the marginal density is informative; otherwise it does not (scopus:2-s2.0-84863338319) [abs].
- Reading for us (R): inactive is a region class that expands; faint cracks share appearance with background and fingers, so the teacher's crack region does not expand into the misses. Matches SYNTHESIS §1 (E: 38% of pool crack is a true miss no rule reached).

## 4. Verdict
Source of each condition: [F] full text read, [A] abstract only.

**Conditions under which the literature reports that unlabelled data helps segmentation (R):**
1. Labels scarce for the task. Same-encoder gains +6 to +11 mIoU at 92-316 labels, +2.5 to +3 at full benchmark labels [F] (doi:10.1109/tpami.2025.3528453). At low labels, thin and tail classes gain as much or more than head classes (Cityscapes 1/32: pole +5.7, tail +10.6 with Mean Teacher) [F] (arxiv:2110.05474).
2. Pool i.i.d. with the labelled set and several times larger. A non-i.i.d. extra pool on top of all labels gives +0.3 to +1.6 [F] (doi:10.1109/tpami.2025.3528453 §4.5); class-mismatched pools can hurt [F] (arxiv:1804.09170).
3. Target classes that expand under augmentation [A] (arxiv:2010.03622): region classes, not faint lines that resemble background.
4. A weak baseline. Tuned baselines and transfer cut reported SSL gains by more than half [F] (arxiv:1804.09170); inadequate baselines inflate claims [A] (doi:10.1007/978-3-031-72114-4_47). In-domain self-supervised gains are large over scratch (VasoMIM +9.9 DSC [F]) and small or absent over a same-architecture ImageNet init ([A] doi:10.1007/978-3-031-25082-8_31; [F] arxiv:2402.17611).
5. For self-supervised pretraining: MIM beats contrastive in small, non-object-centric domains ([F] doi:10.1609/aaai.v40i6.42503; [F] arxiv:2605.12430; [A] arxiv:2112.10740; [F] arxiv:2402.17611).
6. For shifted targets: unlabelled images from the target domain itself (crack UDA ~100 target images [A] doi:10.1111/mice.13315; BN re-estimation +2.6, Tent +7.0 mIoU on sim-to-real [F] arxiv:2006.10726), with unstable tuning [A] (arxiv:2306.03536).

**Reading.**
1. **Our label-propagation results are what the literature predicts.** SSL helped where conditions 1-3 held (5% labels; inactive to 20%), and the 5% crack gain is the same relative size as Mean Teacher on Cityscapes pole. At 100% labels the literature's realistic effect is +0.3 to +1.6 mIoU points, i.e. 0.003-0.016 IoU. On the 49 clean cells our crack threshold is the 0.010 floor and the 80%-power MDE is 0.012 at 100% (`eval.md`); Sx100 − A100 crack is −0.002 [−0.007, +0.003]. So our interval excludes the top of the literature range (+0.016) but not the bottom (+0.003): a literature-sized full-label gain could only just show on this test, at best (agreed with evalx, `eval.md` §1.3). +0.02 is excluded at 20/50/100% and +0.01 at 100% (Cs recipe). Scope: BsM was never run at ≥ 50%, and BsM20 − A20 clean crack +0.013 [−0.002, +0.033] does not exclude +0.01 to +0.03. **Inactive is where small effects are truly invisible** (MDE 0.03-0.08). (Withdrawn: my earlier claim that 0.069-0.091 thresholds make +0.01-0.02 unseeable; those were all-50 rows inflated by the noisy cell.)
2. **The user's intuition is self-supervised pretraining, which we never ran.** On EL it was tested once (contrastive SimCLR, full labels; crack comparison rests on n = 5 runs vs n = 1 for CCT) and lost on crack (arxiv:2402.17611). MIM is the best-supported untested variant. Prior against our ImageNet init (S): small, likely n.d. Every thin-structure MIM gain found is over scratch or across architectures; in the one same-regime industrial case (AOI-SSL: 7k unlabelled, 625 labelled, thin wires) the in-domain-MAE ViT-Tiny **lost** the wire class to an ImageNet ResNet18 (43.0 vs 59.1 IoU). The only same-family wins over ImageNet are detection on X-ray (27.9k) and anomaly-guided metal pretraining (120k). Continued pretraining from an ImageNet model (HPT) beats both ImageNet transfer and target-only pretraining on 15 of 16 datasets, mostly classification, with small detection / segmentation gains [F] (doi:10.1109/wacv51458.2022.00112): the design to use if this is tested.
3. **E1's crack gap is mostly missing coverage, not a domain shift.** 75% of E1 crack px sit in 20 scuff + half-cut cells (recall 0.13); training has 12 scuff cells and 0 half-cut cells (`eval.md` §5; judge J0.8-J0.9). UDA, BN re-estimation and Tent cannot add a context the labelled set lacks. The scuff-free part (12 cells, recall 0.29-0.55) could be a covariate shift (E1 CSIR brighter, higher contrast, judge J0.7) where target-image adaptation might help: untested, and the pool is not E1-like (0 of 32 E1 crack cells in a pool-covered module, `lit_scripts/e1_pool_overlap.py`). At 100% labels the unseen-module test cells' inactive recall is 0.426 for both A100 and Sx100 (judge J0.12, `judge_scripts/j8_inact_unseen_module.py`): the one full-label SSL arm does not touch that gap either.
4. **For rare thin defects under scarce labels, ranked by evidence for our regime:**
   1. pixel labels on crack-rich cells (Track D: **oracle-selected** with GT masks; Severstal: 300 + 50 masks beats 3000 image-labelled positives [F], doi:10.1016/j.compind.2021.103459). A model-based selector is the untested deployable step;
   2. a better pretrained initialisation or source (COCO-seg best on EL crack, arxiv:2402.17611; ResNet-50 probe ranks BMRK crack better at low precision, `trackD/verify_probe.md`; but R3 ResNet-50 at 100% was WORSE on inactive, `trackM/results_r3.md`);
   3. copy-paste / synthetic cracks (rare-class gains in natural images, additive with self-training [A], doi:10.1109/cvpr46437.2021.00294; untested on EL);
   4. self-supervised pretraining continued from our ImageNet encoder on the pool (HPT protocol, short schedule [F], doi:10.1109/wacv51458.2022.00112), MIM objective (SparK-style, mask ratio ~0.5), compared with the same ImageNet-init architecture. This is the honest one-shot test of the user's intuition; prior small (S);
   5. for unseen modules: BN re-estimation on E1-like unlabelled cells (S, inference only), then labels for the missing crack appearance;
   6. anomaly models of normal cells (EL evidence is cell-level only);
   7. SAM-family pseudo-labels: not recommended (fail on thin low-contrast structures and fine-tuning does not fix it [A], doi:10.1109/wacv61042.2026.00505).
5. **The pool's most useful role on current evidence** is as a source of cells to label (targeted / active selection) and, if the user wants the intuition tested once, of MIM pretraining. Not more label-propagation SSL at ≥ 20% labels.
