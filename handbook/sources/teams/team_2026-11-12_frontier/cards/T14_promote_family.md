### P044 Foreground Sampling and Spatial Support in Pavement Crack Segmentation
- Ids: doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11088886/v1 (Research Square preprint, 2026, 0 citations). Code: none named.
- Read: sections 3.1 to 3.4, 3.6.2, 3.10, 4.1, 4.3, 4.8, 5.3 to 5.5, 6.
- Claims (R): Crack500 full-resolution scenes, 247 train / 50 val / 200 test; one U-Net from scratch (7.76M parameters, 18 BatchNorm2d); 256 px crops, batch 16, 20,000 updates, AdamW 3e-4, BCE + Dice, flips and rot90, final checkpoint, 3 paired seeds; primary AP at 2 px tolerance (AP2), paired-image bootstrap, Holm, a practical floor of 0.020 fixed beforehand (3.3 to 3.5). Random crops, BatchNorm: AP2 0.8119 +- 0.0020; positive-only crops 0.7890; 10% positive 0.7299 (4.1). Positive-only cropping leaves 47.0% of the training-image area unreachable (abstract). All 18 BatchNorm layers replaced by GroupNorm with 8 groups: random crops 0.84446 +- 0.00255, positive-only 0.78613 (4.3). BatchNorm statistics re-estimated on a matched source change AP2 by -0.008 to +0.006 (4.3).
- Scout claim check: numbers correct. The +0.033 of GroupNorm over BatchNorm is my difference of two reported means; the paper never tests it as a contrast. GroupNorm is a control for the sampling effect, and the authors write that it "changes both running-state dependence and the normalization operator" so the cause cannot be isolated (3.6.2), and that everything is "conditional on one U-Net architecture, one retained Crack500 split, 256-pixel crops, three initialization seeds" (6). The scout's GroupNorm(16) is not the paper's setting (8 groups); at 16 decoder channels it would be one channel per group.
- Our facts (E): we train on whole 512 px cells, so the paper's main finding (crop reachability) does not bind `rcs`, which samples whole cells. Encoder is ImageNet-pretrained with BatchNorm; batch 8 mixes five sources. The w2a arm `ema_wise_posthoc_pf_v11` already writes a `raw_bn` readout: same weights, BatchNorm statistics recomputed on the fold's train images (E card in `bench_wt_w2/bench/methods/`). smp 0.5.0 offers batchnorm, identity, layernorm, instancenorm for decoders, not GroupNorm (E `segmentation_models_pytorch/base/modules.py`), so a small swap function is needed.
- Overlap: no arm changes a normalisation layer. `raw_bn` covers the running-statistics half of the mechanism for free.
- Hypothesis (S): if recomputed BatchNorm statistics move the PF model at all, a decoder without running statistics has a mechanism; if they do not, only the per-sample operator is left and the paper's evidence for that is one scratch network.
- Protocol use (S): a crop-based row (nnU-Net foreground oversampling) should print crop size, positive-crop share and reachable area, as the paper recommends (5.4). One line for the row's info sheet, no build.
- Status: **RUN_AFTER_GATE** (arm `dec_gn_pf`; gate G-BN = `raw_bn` minus `raw` on the w2a ema dumps).

### P117 TRACE: topology-aware reconnection for thin-structure segmentation
- Ids: arXiv 2610.22210 (2026, 0 citations). Code: "will be released upon acceptance" (R abstract): none.
- Read: sections 3.1 to 3.4, 4.1 to 4.5, 5. The numbered equations did not come through the tool.
- Claims (R): a target skeleton ("carrier") is built by topology-preserving thinning. For each carrier point with at least two neighbouring branches in its 3x3 window, the loss finds the valid reconnection among **target-class pixels of that window** whose worst pixel has the lowest centred logit margin, and supervises that worst pixel together with the carrier point (3.1, 3.4). nnU-Net training, weight chosen from {0.1, 0.3, 0.6, 1.0} on validation Dice (4.2). DeepCrack (270 / 30 / 237): Dice 82.49 to 84.53, clDice 89.48 to 90.60, Betti error 5.21 to 4.95; Skeleton Recall 83.89 / 90.42 / 5.10; Betti Matching 84.28 (Table 1). With CS2-Net on DeepCrack: Dice 82.78 to 85.20, Betti error 6.16 to 3.69; Skeleton Recall 84.26 / 4.74 (Table 3). Ablation ladder on DRIVE from Skeleton Recall to TRACE: Dice 82.18 to 82.38, clDice 81.89 to 82.65, Betti error 97.0 to 79.3 (Table 5). Limitation: annotation fragments and loops are kept as carrier points (Fig. 5).
- Scout claim check: numbers correct. Evidence check: tables give mean +- sd over test images (sd about 10 Dice on DeepCrack); no seed count; one run per method as far as the text shows. The edge over Skeleton Recall is 0.2 to 0.9 Dice. DeepCrack cracks are many pixels wide.
- Our facts (E): labels are 1 to 2 px wide, so the carrier is nearly the label and the window holds almost no alternative route: as published, the term reduces to a pointwise penalty on skeleton points, which is the Skeleton Recall family. Skeleton Recall at weight 0.1 failed its replication on the sealed sets by an inactive guard and a precision guard (E `log.md` 14 Nov 05:58); `skr01_pf_v11` is training as a table row. PF finds 0.886 of crack components of at least 20 px (any hit) and 0.811 at 50% coverage; relR@2 0.799 (E `results/w1c_read/read.txt`). Of the components both seeds miss (88 and 95 of 989), a 2-seed ensemble recovers 0 (E `results/stageA/A2_P1_G5.md`): the misses are systematic, not flicker. How much of the missed crack px are gaps inside found cracks, as against whole missed cracks, has not been measured. A gap of 4 px or less between two found segments is invisible to relR@2.
- Overlap: `skr01_pf_v11` (row, training), `clce_crack` (wave 2b, "run late"), `scnp_cedice`, `cc_dicece_crack`, `coletra_crack`. All four wave-2 arms claim continuity or short-component recall; none has a census of what is missed.
- Hypothesis (S): with the candidate support widened to the label dilated by 1 px, the term rewards the easiest connected route within the label's jitter range. It can raise relR@2 only by the share of missed px that are gaps longer than 4 px inside found cracks.
- Status: **RUN_AFTER_GATE** (arm `trace_crack`). First gate is the CPU read `gap_census`, which should run now because it also orders the four wave-2 arms above.

### P166 Surgical Fine-Tuning Improves Adaptation to Distribution Shifts
- Ids: arXiv 2210.11466 (ICLR 2023, 331 citations). Code: not named in the sections read.
- Read: sections 2, 2.1, 3.2, 4.1, 4.2, 6, A.4.
- Claims (R): setting = a model trained on a source distribution is fine-tuned on a **small labelled target set**; "surgical" = tune one block, freeze the rest (2). Seven shifts, ResNet-26 / ResNet-50 classifiers and CLIP ViT-B/16, 3 seeds, learning rate swept over 3 values and early stopping, both on held-out target data (2.1, A.4). Best block against tuning everything: CIFAR-C 82.8 against 79.9, ImageNet-C 51.6 against 50.7, Living-17 93.2 against 92.8, CIFAR-Flip 93.8 against 85.9 (Table 4, "Cross-Val" row). Input-level shifts favour the first block, output-level shifts the last layer (Fig. 2). The gap shrinks as target data grows (Fig. 3 text). A per-tensor criterion, relative gradient norm (Auto-RGN), is best among single-run methods (4.1, 4.2).
- Scout claim check: wrong setting. The scout proposes to hold layer3 and layer4 at ImageNet during the main training and predicts a gain on E1. The paper has no zero-shot result: every number is accuracy after supervised tuning on target data, with the learning rate and the stopping point chosen on target data. Its setting is our T3b track (K labelled cells of a new module type), not the main model.
- Our facts (E): PF already keeps layer3 / layer4 near ImageNet (cosine to init 0.93 / 0.94 at 20k steps; E `T1_recipes_spec.md` arm 4). The two points we have on that dose curve: HOUSE (erased) to PF gives -0.011 to -0.017 strict crack IoU in distribution (E `W1C_READ.md` 3) and E1 inactive 0.05 to 0.24 (E BRIEF 3). `encoder_spd` and its control (w2a, training) vary encoder preservation per tensor. `training.encoder_pf` builds two parameter groups only (E `scripts/train.py` l.136-147), so a per-block freeze needs one new key.
- Overlap: `encoder_spd` (per-tensor version of the same question for the main model); T13 arm 3 `t3b_fewshot_rule` (rules R0, R1, R1b, R2 for K labelled target cells; no partial-freeze rule).
- Hypothesis (S): on the LOSO-2 proxy with K = 4 or 16 labelled cells of the held-out source, tuning only the stem and layer1, or only the decoder, forgets less and may match or beat tuning everything.
- Not built: the main-training freeze. PF sits at cosine 0.93; a full freeze moves that to 1.0; the expected in-distribution effect is zero or negative, and the transfer claim has no instrument (Y3, Y9). P353 (freeze layer4) and P309 (encoder BatchNorm frozen) were to ride on this card: they stay parked with it.
- Status: **RUN_AFTER_GATE** (arm `t3b_surgical_rules`: two more rules inside T13 arm 3).

### P184 The Treasure Beneath Multiple Annotations: An Uncertainty-Aware Edge Detector (UAED)
- Ids: doi:10.1109/cvpr52729.2023.01488; arXiv 2303.11828. Code: https://github.com/ZhouCX117/UAED (R abstract; T4 found no licence on the page).
- Read: sections 3 to 3.3, 4.1, 4.2, 4.4 to 4.6.
- Claims (R): BSDS500 has 4 to 9 annotators per image, Multicue 5 to 6 (4.1). One randomly drawn annotation supervises the mean branch; a second decoder predicts a variance that is supervised by the pixel-wise variance of the label set; the BCE is up-weighted where predicted variance is high (3.2, 3.3). BSDS ablation, ODS / OIS / AP: baseline (labels fused at 0.3) 0.824 / 0.841 / 0.875; + variance branch 0.825 / 0.843 / 0.884; + variance supervision 0.829 / 0.846 / 0.892; + weighting 0.829 / 0.847 / 0.892 (Table 3). Other backbones: +0.003 to +0.007 ODS (Table 6). Batch 4, Adam 1e-4. Seeds not stated for BSDS.
- Scout claim check: "SOTA on BSDS" is right; the effect is +0.005 ODS with 4 to 9 independent annotators, and the uncertainty weighting itself adds 0.000 to 0.002 ODS. The sweeper's reason, "label version moved crack IoU by 0.05 to 0.08, so a second annotator view is the largest known lever", misreads our fact: that number is the gain from replacing the old labels, not from having two.
- Our facts (E): the two label versions are not two annotators. Of 234 pool crack cells, 142 (60.7%) have identical crack masks; in the 90 changed cells the chance that one version has crack 1 px beside the other's crack is 0.0054 (E `harness/x2_check/p1/p1.out`). The revision is "a cleaning, not an independent re-annotation": dotted labels joined, +51% crack px on changed train images (E handbook `03_datasets_and_splits.md` 3.1, 3.2). Training on the old masks instead of the revised ones cost 0.082 crack IoU scored on the revised labels (10% labels; E same file 3.3). So sampling the 2021 version as an "annotator" re-injects a third of missing crack px half of the time, and the variance target would be zero on every shared trace.
- Training arm: closed by these two measurements. Not specified.
- What is left (S): the px the revision added are a human-made difficulty stratum. Recall on stable px against recall on added px is a cheap descriptive table for the benchmark's label-noise section (the scout's "benchmark uncertainty view", made concrete).
- Status: **PROTOCOL** (CPU read `label_version_strata`). P293 (D-Persona), parked behind this paper, stays parked.

D0: 0.60 of predicted skeleton points lie within 1 px of the ridge (E `W1C_READ.md` 5).
- The paper-faithful arm (jitter large segments of structure classes and inactive): the paper's own average gain, 0.002 to 0.009 mIoU, is below M = 0.010 for mIoU-R13. Not built (Y9).
- Status: **RUN_AFTER_GATE** (arm `label_jitter_crack`; gate = `soft_band_jml` minus `jml_hard` passes in the w2a read). If the soft band is null, this is parked with it.
