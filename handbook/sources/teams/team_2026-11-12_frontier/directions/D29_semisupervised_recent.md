# D29: Semi-supervised segmentation, 2023 to 2026, at high label fractions or with foundation models

Scout: D29 literature agent, 13 Nov 2026. Tags: R = what the paper measured, E = what our files measured (from BRIEF), S = my judgement.

## Bottom line (S)
Our SSL null was Mean Teacher (EMA of itself, online) with hard or tolerant pseudo-labels on a ResNet-34 U-Net++. The recent literature says three things are different when SSL works at full labels:
1. **The teacher is stronger than the student, and fixed.** Noisy Student, Data Distillation, Zoph et al. and ensemble distillation all use an offline teacher that beats the student (bigger model, ensemble, or TTA). Our only clear win is the 5-seed ensemble (+0.014 to +0.018 crack IoU). Distilling it through soft labels on the 478 train cells plus the 11.9k pool is the cheapest untested arm, and soft ensemble targets also blur the 1 to 2 px label jitter.
2. **The backbone is a large pretrained ViT.** UniMatch V2 shows +2.2 to +2.5 mIoU on Pascal with ALL 1,464 labels when the encoder is DINOv2-S/B, and +0.3 to +1.6 when full labelled sets get extra truly unlabelled data (R). We have never fine-tuned a ViT-B/L with or without SSL. Main risk: 14 px patches against 1 to 2 px cracks, so a high-resolution CNN branch is needed.
3. **The unlabelled data is used for a domain gap, not more of the same.** UDA methods (MIC) and mixed-domain SSL (SynFoC) aim at a target shift. Our pool has 1,152 multi half-cells, the cell type behind most of E1's loss, and nobody has run target-aimed self-training on them.

Most 2024 to 2026 SSL papers (crack, vessel, medical) remain Mean Teacher or CPS variants tested at 1 to 20% labels. They are rejected below.

## Minimum experiments (one per arm; all on 100% labels, house or PF as the baseline)
1. **Ensemble distillation (#1, #2, #4, #5).** Teacher: 5-seed PF ensemble + dihedral TTA, soft probabilities saved for train 478 and the 11.9k pool (about 0.5 GPU-h). Arm A: PF student on train soft targets only. Arm B: A + pool soft targets with the strong aug from #2. Baseline: PF single model (5 seeds) and the 5-seed ensemble. Metrics: crack IoU and relF1@2 on clean 49, inactive IoU, mIoU_rob, E1 crack relF1@2 and inactive IoU. Kill: B below the PF single-model mean + 0.010 crack IoU AND no E1 gain over PF. Success line: B reaches the ensemble (0.485+ crack IoU at argmax) as a single model. About 10 GPU-h per arm for 5 seeds.
2. **UniMatch V2 hybrid (#3).** Arm A: DINOv2-B + DPT + stride-2 CNN skip, supervised on 478 cells. Arm B: A + UniMatch V2 on the 11.9k pool. 3 seeds, 40k steps, about 10 GPU-h per run. Compare B v A (does SSL help a ViT at full labels here?) and B v house. Kill: B - A < +0.01 crack relF1@2 and < +0.02 inactive IoU, and B below house crack IoU by more than 0.02.
3. **MIC on half-cells (#6).** Arm: house PF + MIC loss with the half-cell pool as target. Baseline: PF. Metric: E1 crack relF1@2, E1 inactive IoU, half-cut false-alarm rate on F51/H36; test clean 49 as a non-inferiority check (crack IoU drop no more than 0.01). Kill: E1 crack relF1@2 gain < +0.03 over PF at 5 seeds.
4. **GKD pool distillation (#8).** Stage 1 on the pool (DINOv2-L teacher, about 6 GPU-h), stage 2 house PF. Baseline: PF from ImageNet. Kill: E1 crack relF1@2 and inactive IoU both within noise of PF, and test crack IoU not better.

## Notable papers rejected (one line each)
- SemiVL, doi:10.1007/978-3-031-72933-1_15 (code google-research/semivl): CLIP text has no meaning for crack, inactive, splice in EL; the mechanism depends on language grounding. Benchmark value only.
- AllSpark, doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.00348: regenerates labelled features from unlabelled ones by cross-attention; it targets scarce labels, gains reported at small splits only.
- SemiOVS, doi:10.1016/j.knosys.2025.114289: pseudo-labels OOD images with an open-vocabulary model; there is no open-vocabulary model for EL defects.
- CW-BASS v2, arxiv:2608.12773, and PixCon, arxiv:2607.03068: pseudo-label selection and contrastive refinements on top of UniMatch V2 with gains of +0.2 to +1.5 mIoU on saturated benchmarks; only worth it after #3 works. CW-BASS v2's held-out reliability gate is a useful diagnostic for any teacher (measure Pr[correct | conf >= tau] on val before choosing a threshold).
- PrevMatch (WACV 2026), ScaleMatch (AAAI 2025), BoundMatch arxiv:2503.23519, CorrMatch (CVPR 2024), "When Confidence Fails" (ICCV 2025): refinements of weak-to-strong pseudo-labelling; same family as our null.
- Crack SSL: Cross teacher pseudo supervision (doi:10.1016/j.aei.2023.102279), Xiang et al. 2023 contrastive + CPS (doi:10.1016/j.measurement.2023.113091), Asadi Shamsabadi 2024 (doi:10.1016/j.autcon.2023.105181), dual-student MT 2025: all Mean Teacher or CPS at low label fractions on pavement or concrete.
- Vessel SSL (pseudo-label filtering, Symmetry 2025): same family, low label fractions.
- Mixed-domain SSL with intermediate domains, doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.01106: close to SynFoC's setting but without the foundation model; keep as a fallback reference.
- SemiSAM+ (doi:10.1016/j.media.2025.103733), learnable-prompt SAM KD (doi:10.1109/tmi.2025.3530097): SAM as a promptable teacher for organs; SAM masks are poor on 1-2 px cracks, and these papers report low-label results only.
- Crack UDA with Bayesian self-training and spatial priors, doi:10.1111/mice.13315: concrete walls, overlaps MIC's slot; code not verified. A second-line option for E1.
- Depth Anything, doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.00987: shows a frozen large teacher + strongly perturbed student on 62M unlabelled images beats labelled-only training, with feature alignment to DINOv2. This is the same mechanism as arms 1 and 4 on a different task, so I cite it as support, not as a separate arm.

## Gaps
- Semantic Scholar was rate-limited for the whole session, so snippet-level evidence (numbers for SSL at full labels in thin-structure domains) is missing. No paper found that tests SSL at 100% labels on thin 1-2 px structures.
- No 2025-2026 SSL paper with DINOv3 at full labels was found; DINO-Mix is low-label only.
- Codes for Noisy Student, Data Distillation, Zoph, Noothout, ConformalSAM, GKD, SemiCurv are not verified (marked "none verified" or "not verified").
- Sub-directions that deserve their own search: (a) ViT segmenters with high-resolution CNN branches for thin structures (needed before #3 can work); (b) diffusion or ControlNet synthesis of EL half-cut cells; (c) calibration and uncertainty metrics for the benchmark (RSS-style).
