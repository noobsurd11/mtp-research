OP-C (cross-fitted class biases) is the comparator for arms that shift class priors (ruling L3); its definition comes from stage B item B1 and is "pending review" until `harness/REVIEW_STAGEB.md` exists: read `results/stageB/B1_classbias_xfit.md` and state its status as you find it.

Screen = 2 seeds, table row = 5 seeds. A crack gain counts only if it holds at OP-A and OP-W inside the width band, with delta at least M and the interval above 0. A screen flag is not a result. A read never mixes machines. Inactive IoU promotes no trained arm at 2 seeds; inactive arms are read by region counts or at 5 seeds (finding Y1 of `cards/WAVE3_REVIEW.md`, ruling L4).
