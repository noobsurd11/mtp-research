## 1. Reading rule (fixed before any number; written 04:50, while the W1-C1 run was computing records and before any output was read)
Definitions (as built; the spec's pseudo-code made concrete where it was open):
- A/A split: two disjoint k-seed sets of ONE arm (`house_r34_unetpp_pf_v11`) on ONE machine, read on the same 532 out-of-fold cells as if B were an arm and A the baseline. k = 2: all 15 splits of 5 seeds (cv02) or 3 of 4 seeds (gaint); k = 1: 10 (cv02) or 6 (gaint). Operating points OP-A, OP-F, OP-W from `crossfit_op.operating_points` with the arm as its own baseline (so OP-W targets the seed's own width).
- Bootstrap: 2,000 draws, seed 0, 330 groups (`cv5v2_groups.tsv`). Seed stages: 'fold' = `crossfit_op.boot_pair` as used today (one seed per fold and arm; the new function reproduces boot_pair exactly, unit-tested); 'set' = the spec's replacement (per fold and arm, k seeds drawn with replacement, k virtual seeds, metric = their mean); 'fixed' = no seed stage (groups only).
- Promotion of a split (the screen rule as applied to an arm): |delta| >= M and the 95% CI beyond 0 on the same side, M = max(0.01, 2 x SE of that split). Counted in either orientation, because the orientation of an A/A split is arbitrary.
- r = RMS of the 15 A/A deltas / mean bootstrap SE of the 15 splits. RMS (sd about the known A/A mean 0) instead of the sd about the sample mean, because each split's sign is arbitrary. The sd is printed beside it.
- Part (d), OP-A, per seed: SE_flat (cells resampled), SE_group (330 groups), DEFF = (SE_group / SE_flat)^2; SE_group+seed for the 5-seed arm ('fold' stage).
- Part (e), per metric and operating point: sigma2_seed = var over seeds (ddof 1) of the pooled per-seed metric; Vg(k) = mean over the k-splits of the groups-only bootstrap variance of the A/A delta; Vs(k) = mean over the k-splits of delta^2 (seed spread; at k = 1 it equals 2 sigma2_seed); V(k) = Vg(k) + Vs(k) (the critic's groups + seeds decomposition, as PROTOCOL 3.2's 0.031 / 0.022); a, b from V(1), V(2) exactly (b = 2 (V1 - V2), a = 2 V2 - V1); MDE(k) = 2.8 sqrt(a + b / k), k = 2, 3, 5.

Rules (spec section 1 and Y1, thresholds unchanged), read at OP-A on the four headline metrics (crack IoU, crack relF1@2, inactive IoU, mIoU-R13), stage 'fold':
1. Promotion share <= 1 of 15 and r in [0.7, 1.5]: keep M = max(0.01, 2 x SE_AA).
2. r > 1.5: M = max(0.01, 2 x RMS of the 15 A/A deltas) and every arm CI is widened by r before the "CI low > 0" check.
3. r < 0.7: the 'set' stage replaces the seed stage only if its own r is in [0.7, 1.5] and its promotion share stays <= 1 of 15; otherwise the current stage stays (M conservative). Per the critic, a replacement is made for all arms at once through the new function; if the four metrics point to different branches, the lead decides.
4. Primary of the item: promotion share of the 15 k = 2 splits, crack relF1@2, OP-A.
5. Inactive (spec): if MDE(2) for inactive IoU > 0.08, inactive is never a screen primary; it is read on 5 seeds only.
6. Inactive (critic Y1, L4): if 2 x sqrt(a) > 0.05 for inactive IoU (OP-A), pooled inactive IoU is never a screen primary for a trained arm; trained inactive arms promote on the count-based readouts of Y1.
7. Part (d): DEFF < 1.2 for every headline metric: grouping costs little. DEFF > 2 for any: every flat cell bootstrap in the protocol (L-test) is marked anti-conservative where cells share modules.
8. M per machine: reported as max(0.01, 2 x mean SE over the k = 2 splits) under the adopted stage, with the canonical split {5,6} v {7,8} beside it (the value `bench_read_w1.py --aa` would print).
Known limit (S, stated now): an A/A has no true effect, so its group part contains only cell-by-seed noise; the effect heterogeneity of a real arm across modules is invisible to it. The A/A value of a is therefore a lower bound for a real arm. A supplementary real contrast (W1-C2 house minus W1-C1 PF, seeds 5, 6, OP-A, groups only) is printed beside it for that reason; it decides nothing.

## 3. Outcome under the rules
1. **Primary (crack relF1@2, OP-A, stage fold): 0 of 15 A/A splits promoted.** The screen rule does not over-promote on any headline metric at OP-A or OP-W (0 of 15 each; OP-F also 0 of 15).
2. Branch per headline metric (rules 1 to 3):
   - crack IoU: r 0.74, in [0.7, 1.5], promotion 0 of 15: **keep M = max(0.01, 2 x SE_AA)**.
   - relF1@2 (r 0.55), inactive IoU (0.49), mIoU-R13 (0.48): r < 0.7, so the 'set' stage was tried: its r is 0.66, 0.59, 0.58, all still below 0.7. **The 'set' stage fails its adoption check, so the current seed stage stays** (M conservative). No metric points to a replacement, so the critic's "all arms at once" condition does not arise: **the bootstrap is unchanged for every arm.**
3. **M on cv02 (W1-C1), OP-A and OP-W: crack IoU 0.010, relF1@2 0.010, mIoU-R13 0.010 (all three at the 0.01 floor; 2 SE = 0.0064, 0.0078, 0.0062), inactive IoU 0.049.** OP-F: 0.016, 0.024, 0.049, 0.010. The canonical {5,6} v {7,8} split gives the same values (inactive 0.050).
4. Rule 5 (spec): MDE(2) for inactive IoU = 0.052, below 0.08, so the spec's rule does not bar inactive as a screen primary.
5. **Rule 6 (critic Y1, part (e)) is unusable as specified, and I leave the decision to the lead.** Reason (E, and algebra): when V(k) is averaged over all splits of one set of seeds, both of its parts are exact U-statistics of the per-seed values, so V(1) = 2 V(2) identically and a = 2 V(2) - V(1) = 0 for any data. Measured: a = 1e-18 or smaller and Vg(2)/Vg(1) = 0.50 for every metric and operating point. An A/A has no true effect, so it cannot contain the part that does not shrink with seeds (effect heterogeneity across modules); 2 sqrt(a) > 0.05 can never fire from an A/A.
   What the data can say instead (S): the groups-only SE of a real contrast minus the A/A groups-only SE estimates that part. HOUSE - PF on inactive: 0.0255 against 0.0142, so the non-shrinking variance lies between 0.0255^2 - 0.0142^2 and 0.0255^2, that is 2 sqrt(a) between 0.042 and 0.051, straddling the critic's 0.05. PFV1 - PF: 0.0085, below the A/A value (two arms trained with the same seed numbers and almost the same labels share their cell-level noise), so no such part is visible. One 2-seed contrast per arm type cannot fix a; the lead decides whether Y1's count-based policy (L4) stands on its own, which it can.
6. Rule 7 (part (d)): DEFF lies between 1.2 and 2 for inactive (1.32) and mIoU-R13 (1.46), below 1.2 for crack IoU (1.14) and relF1@2 (1.08), on both machines. Neither fixed reading applies; module grouping costs little for crack metrics and about a third more variance for inactive and mIoU. The L-test marking is the lead's call.

S, for the lead (outside the fixed rules, nothing applied):
- Today's seed stage ('fold') overstates the SE of a 2-seed contrast by about 1.3 to 2 times against the empirical A/A spread (r 0.48 to 0.74). The spec's candidate ('set') narrows it but not enough. The groups-only stage ('fixed') gives r 0.82 to 1.29 at OP-A, i.e. it is calibrated for the A/A (seed noise here is mostly cell-level, which the group resampling already sees; for crack IoU, r 1.29, a global seed effect remains). Adopting it would lower M only for inactive (0.049 to 0.028); crack and mIoU M stay at the 0.01 floor in every stage. Any change must be one function for all arms (critic), so it is the lead's decision.
- Against the critic's planning numbers: the inactive contrast SE at 2 seeds is 0.025 on cv5v2 / v1.1 (fold stage), not 0.038 (B6, PROTOCOL 3.2), so M_inactive is about 0.05, not 0.07 to 0.08. Y9 comparisons should use M = 0.010 (crack IoU, relF1@2, mIoU-R13) and 0.049 (inactive) on cv02.
