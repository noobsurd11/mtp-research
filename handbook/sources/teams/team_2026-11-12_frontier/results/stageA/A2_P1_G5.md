## 1. Reading rule (fixed before any number; written about 05:10, before any tool ran on real dumps (driver started 05:11:34))
Definitions as built:
- Teacher = the out-of-fold 2-seed PF ensemble (`house_r34_unetpp_pf_v11`, seeds 5 and 6, mean softmax from the dumped `logp`, argmax), one derived dump per held-out fold. D4 views are not part of it (no `msc_tta` dumps exist).
- Singles = the same two seeds. Delta = ensemble minus the mean over the two seeds of each seed's pooled metric (`crossfit_op.boot_pair`, two-stage group bootstrap, 330 groups, 2,000 draws, seed 0, one seed per fold and arm).
- OP-A = argmax. OP-W = the ensemble's crack bias cross-fitted per fold so that its width ratio matches the singles' mean argmax width ratio on the fit cells (the "matched operating point"); the singles stay at argmax (their own width).
- (b) seed complementarity: GT crack components >= 20 px, missed = no predicted crack px within 2 px (m0 any-hit), OP-A; P(missed by one seed | missed by the other), both orders, pooled and per source; the same for inactive regions >= 100 px (missed = no overlap). Also: of the components both seeds miss, how many the ensemble hits.
- (c) per source: (a) restricted to the source's cells; intervals only for sources with >= 40 cells.

Rules:
1. **P1 (pool_ens_distill gate; T9 rule with the critic's fix Y9):** P1 opens iff delta strict crack IoU at OP-W >= M, where M is the measured margin for strict crack IoU on the machine of the read (A1). If it does not open, arm 1 is parked (T9: it would repeat the closed same-strength self-training), unless D4 views add at least +0.005 on top of the ensemble; that clause cannot be evaluated now (no D4 dumps) and is stated as open. relF1@2 at OP-W is reported beside it but no longer decides (the critic's fix names strict crack IoU only).
2. **M:** cv02 M for strict crack IoU at OP-A and OP-W = 0.010 (E, A1 on W1-C1, at the 0.01 floor). gaint M is not measured yet (A/A needs W1-G2 seeds 7, 8). M is never below 0.01 (floor of the rule). For the gaint read the outcome is stated at M = 0.010 and as a function of M (opens iff M <= delta).
3. **P1 (b):** if P(missed by one seed | missed by the other) for crack components is above 0.9, arm 1 still runs, its expected gain is set to the low end and relR@2 is not expected to move.
4. **G5 (b) (ens_kd_jml gate, as written in T2):** the OOF 2-seed ensemble (mean softmax, argmax = OP-A) beats the mean of the two single seeds by >= 0.005 on strict crack IoU or on relF1@2: pass; otherwise drop ens_kd_jml (nothing to distil). G5 (a) holds by construction (PF dumps with `logp` for all 5 folds). G5 (c) (teacher not a copy of the labels, on 50 training cells of fold 0) needs in-sample teacher maps, which no dump holds: it is not run here (section 4).
5. Y9 general rule (S, for the lead; not a gate written for G5): an arm whose teacher edge is below M cannot pass a screen read with margin M, so a null of ens_kd_jml would then close nothing. The numbers below let the lead apply it.
6. Y8: W1-G (gaint) is exploration; the outcome is a result after the same script, unchanged, reproduces it on W1-C1 (cv02 seeds 5, 6), where M = 0.010 is measured.
7. The critic's order stands whatever the outcome: pool_ens_distill is built only after ens_kd_jml is read or parked; the two are never run together.

## 3. Outcome under the rules
1. **P1 (rule 1, critic's Y9 fix): the 2-seed ensemble beats the single models at OP-W by +0.0116 strict crack IoU on both machines.**
   - cv02 (W1-C1, M = 0.010 measured, A1): 0.0116 >= 0.010, **P1 opens.** CI [0.0080, 0.0155].
   - gaint (W1-G, M not measured yet): **opens at M = 0.010** and for any M <= 0.0116; closes for M > 0.0116. M cannot be below 0.01 (floor). CI [0.0068, 0.0168].
   - Y8: W1-C1 reproduces W1-G (same delta, same sign of every headline contrast), so this is a result.
   - The D4 clause is not needed (P1 opens without it) and could not be evaluated (no D4 dumps).
2. **P1 (b) (rule 3): mean P(miss | other seed missed) for crack components = 0.814 (W1-G) and 0.804 (W1-C1), not above 0.9.** The rule does not set the expected gain to the low end. Observed beside it (E): the ensemble never finds a crack component that both seeds miss (0 of 88, 0 of 95) or an inactive region both miss (0 of 62, 0 of 70). Its edge comes from components only one seed finds and from pixel placement, not from new detections; CSIR (the unseen-module source) has the highest conditional miss share (0.85 to 0.94).
3. **G5 (b) (rule 4, as written): passes on both machines.** OP-A delta strict crack IoU +0.0116 and relF1@2 +0.0108 (W1-G), +0.0116 and +0.0106 (W1-C1), both >= 0.005. G5 (a) holds. **G5 (c) is not run** (section 4), so ens_kd_jml's gate is open on (a) and (b) and pending on (c).
4. Rule 5 (Y9, S for the lead): the teacher edge (0.0116) is above M (0.010) by 0.0016, and its CI low (0.0068 gaint, 0.0080 cv02) is below M. A student read with margin M at 2 seeds must recover at least 86% of the teacher's own edge to pass; T9's drop rule kills arm 1 below a third of it (0.0039). So the gate opens, but by the specs' own numbers a pass of either distillation arm at M is unlikely (S): T9 expects +0.000 to +0.012 strict crack IoU, the top of which only just reaches M.
5. Order (rule 7): ens_kd_jml is read (or parked) first; pool_ens_distill waits for that; they never run together.
