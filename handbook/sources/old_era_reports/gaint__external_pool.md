## Summary

* **Filtering:** 11,993 files, each a distinct cell, and all are listed in `_inventory.json`. 55 files are dropped because their cell
  is a labelled image: 48 train, 6 val, 1 test. **11,938 are kept**, converted once to 512 × 512 grayscale, and listed in
  `data/splits/external_unlabeled.txt`.
  * None of the files is corrupt or near-constant, and every written file re-reads identically.
  * Decision: only exact cell-ID matches are dropped, as asked. 196 kept cells come from val modules and 88 from test modules. They
    are not removed, because the official split itself shares modules: 20 / 54 val and 24 / 50 test images come from modules that
    also have train images.
* **Mismatches with the labelled images**, from the tables below:
  1. **Sources.** The pool has only ARTS (2,734) and CSIR (9,204) cells. 205 of the 478 labelled train images (43 %) are CFVS, BMRK
     or SDLE, which have no counterpart in the pool.
  2. **Native 512 px images (6,834, 57 % of the pool) have about half the contrast of the labelled images.**
     * The median per-image sd is 26–29 against 43–50 for labelled mono / multi cells.
     * Their 1st percentile is higher: 55–87 against 22–28.
     * The 1200 px images, area-downsampled here, are closer: sd 47–55, p1 20.
     * KS D on sd vs labelled train: 0.34–0.45 for the 512 px groups, 0.08–0.20 for the 1200 px groups.
  3. **Sharpness (mean |Laplacian|)** differs most for multi cells and for ARTS.
     * External multi 4.3–4.8 vs labelled multi 7.5 (D 0.60).
     * ARTS 5.4 vs 9.4 (D 0.72).
     * Half-cells 2.9 (D 0.75).
  4. **Brightness:** CSIR matches (median mean 141 vs 142, D 0.09). ARTS external cells are brighter (170 vs 134, D 0.46); the
     largest gap is ARTS mono (D 0.67, but only 22 labelled ARTS mono train images).
  5. **Colour:** none. Both sets are near-grey RGB, and no group has a median share of coloured pixels above 0.
* **The pool is used as converted.** No intensity harmonisation was applied, so step 3's MT runs see these differences.

Written as 8-bit PNG to `data/unlabeled/images_512/<CELL_ID>.png` (not in git).
* Corrupt / unreadable source files: **0**. Every written file was re-read and matched (size 512 × 512, mode L, identical pixels). Near-constant images (sd < 1): 0.
