## 2. Detail

### P11, 10% labels (cv02, 3 seeds; `results/p11/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dm − A (pipeline check) | −0.007 | +0.000 | −0.005 |
| Cm − Dm (loss helps) | +0.009 | **+0.071** | **+0.018** |
| Cm − A (beats supervised) | +0.002 | **+0.071** | **+0.013** |
- Bootstrap: Cm − Dm inactive [+0.020, +0.121], survives top-3 removal. Cm − A inactive [−0.009, +0.158], +0.028 without top 3. Cm − A relF1@2 [+0.005, +0.024].
- External pool (Cmx): no loss effect.
- Gap to 100% labels closed by Cm: crack IoU 3%, inactive 53%, relF1@2 21%.

### P12, 5% labels (cv02 3 seeds; gaint replication, seeds 3–5; `results/p12/README.md`)
| Contrast | crack IoU | inactive IoU | crack relF1@2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dm5 − A5c (pipeline check) | −0.017 | −0.015 | −0.025 |
| Cm5 − Dm5 (loss helps) | **+0.023** | +0.018 | **+0.040** |
| Cm5 − A5c (beats supervised) | +0.006 | +0.003 | +0.015 |
| gCm5 − gDm5 (gaint) | **+0.047** | +0.077 | **+0.078** |
| gCm5 − A5 (gaint) | **+0.029** | +0.068 | **+0.058** |
- The loss's crack gain about cancels the loss-off handicap (Dm5 − A5c crack −0.017 [−0.024, −0.010]). The handicap is consistent with BN mixing; P13 tests that.
- gaint passes the SSL-over-supervised arithmetic (robust over cells: crack IoU [+0.015, +0.040], inactive [+0.022, +0.130]); cv02 does not. Locked rules give gaint no say. The machines disagree with 3 seeds each.
- Inactive is unmeasurable at 5% (seed sd up to 0.14; 2 of 23 labelled cells have it).

### E1, task metrics and the unseen-module extension (`results/e1/README.md`)
- 106 extra BenchmarkEL cells from 67 modules we never trained on (1 shared with our 582; 1 with the external pool).
- Every model loses most crack performance there: L3 100% crack IoU 0.471 → 0.152.
- Tolerant pseudo-labels (P11, P12): on unseen modules, crack false alarms up by ~0.10 of crack-free cells; inactive cell IoU up (+0.07 to +0.21).
