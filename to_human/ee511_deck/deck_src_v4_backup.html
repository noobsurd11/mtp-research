What we learned about measuring

Same predictions, four crack label conventions: strict IoU moves with line width; relF1@2 barely moves.
  • The label release alone shifts strict crack IoU by +0.045
  • 24 physical modules sat in two folds: regrouped by module
  • The old test set was opened 19 times: final models now read once on sealed E1

Conclusion

Fair measurement first
module-grouped splits, tolerant metric, sealed reads
Two EL-paper models below our baseline
−0.08 to −0.09 crack relF1@2, equal training
Small confirmed gain on unseen modules
+0.043, low end near 0
Target-layout labels help most
+0.22 few-shot (a different contrast)

Limitations

  • E1: 101 of 106 cells from one source
  • Inactive unresolved (10 E1 cells)
  • On E1 only gains above ~0.07 are detectable
  • No half-cut cells in the training pool
  • Next: few labels per new layout; thermal imaging; severity
