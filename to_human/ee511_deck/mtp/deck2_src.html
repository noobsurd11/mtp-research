The problem: thin, rare defects
Problem
  • EL image: bright = working silicon, dark = no current
  • Crack: 0.19% of training pixels, labels 1 to 2 px wide
  • Inactive area: present in 42 of 478 training cells
  • 24 classes, labelled per pixel
Find them, and measure it fairly.
