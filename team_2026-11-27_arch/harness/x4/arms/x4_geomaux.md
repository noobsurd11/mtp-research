# x4_geomaux: training-only geometric crack heads on the decoder output (builder geo)

## What it changes
COMBO (hrnet_w18_unetpp_swad_const_pf_v11) plus three 1x1-conv heads on the final U-Net++ decoder feature map (the 16-channel full-resolution input of the segmentation head), trained from the ground-truth crack (class 14) of each augmented training cell, dropped at inference.
- Targets (src/trainers/geomaux.crack_geom), built per sample in the data workers from the augmented mask through the x2 AuxTargetDataset path (no random draw; image and mask objects unchanged): (a) distance to the nearest crack px, clipped at 10 px and divided by 10 (1 everywhere on a crack-free cell); (b) crack direction (cos 2t, sin 2t) of the skeleton's local tangent (principal axis of the second moments of the skeleton px in a disc of radius 3; skeleton = skeletonize_2d, the scikit-image Zhang-Suen port), copied from the nearest skeleton px to every px within 3 px of the skeleton; (c) local width 2 * EDT_in - 1 px at the nearest skeleton px (EDT_in = distance transform of the crack mask; exact for odd widths, w - 1 for even widths), clipped at 10 px / 10, within 3 px of the skeleton. Ignore px (255) count as non-crack for the targets and are excluded from every loss term.
- Loss (fp32): 0.1 x mean L1 of distance over valid px + 0.1 x mean L1 of (cos, sin) within the direction band + 0.1 x mean L1 of width within the width band (empty band = 0). Added to the house CE + Dice; gradients reach the whole model through the decoder features.
- Heads: separate module 'geomaux' (dist 1 ch, dir 2 ch, width 1 ch, linear outputs, 68 params), initialised under a forked RNG seeded from (seed, 47) so the global RNG is untouched; own optimizer of the model's class with the model optimizer's defaults (Adam, the house lr and weight decay of group 0, house warm-up + constant schedule, no PF multiplier), own gradient clip, checkpointed in latest.pth under models / optimizers / schedulers 'geomaux' (added to weight_avg RESERVED). model.state_dict(), best.pth, SWAD / EMA averages, BN readouts and bench_infer dumps are exactly COMBO-shaped; the dump loads st["models"][weights_key] only, so the dump format is unchanged.
- Motivation: COMBO's missed crack px are crack ends (0.52 of the miss) and interior gaps (0.28) of long cracks with confidently negative margins (cpu/REPORT.md); the heads make the decoder features encode where a crack is, which way it runs and how wide it is, around the whole crack including just beyond its ends. Papers: local shape descriptors doi:10.1038/s41592-022-01711-z; MASTER P028 / P062 (distance-map and orientation heads).
- Keys: training.geomaux.{enabled=true, cls=14, dist_clip=10.0, band=3.0, dir_radius=3, width_clip=10.0, w_dist=0.1, w_dir=0.1, w_width=0.1}. Log: own line 'geomaux geo_dist=... geo_dir=... geo_width=... geo_dir_px=... geo_loss=... at step N' every log window (the step line keeps COMBO's keys).
- Refuses: non-supervised runs, cp, absh, scuff class, Skeleton Recall, aux-target losses, miro, non-smp models.
- Code: src/trainers/geomaux.py; src/trainers/trainer.py (build, step branch, clip / step / schedule, checkpoint keys, log line); scripts/train.py (crack_geom builder added to the aux builders when on); src/trainers/weight_avg.py (RESERVED).
- Run time, E: crack_geom 0.03 s per real 512 px mask on average (120 masks, 28 with crack; 0.10 s for crack-heavy masks), so about 0.06 s per step over the 4 workers: no data bottleneck expected (S). CPU step about 15% slower than COMBO on gaint (76 vs 66 s, loaded machine).
- Precomputed inputs: none on disk (fold-safe by construction: only the run's own augmented train cells).
