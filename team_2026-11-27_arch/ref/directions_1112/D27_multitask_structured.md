## Why this direction fits our data (S)
- 20% of train crack pixels lie in or at inactive regions, and inactive never occurs without a crack (99% touch one). A single softmax must drop one of the two labels on those pixels. That is a containment / adjacency structure a softmax head cannot represent.
- Inactive precision 0.63, E1 inactive IoU 0.05 (house): many false inactive regions. A cell-level "has inactive" signal costs nothing to derive (42 positive train cells).
- Weak classes (gridline 0.283, splice 0.000, edge_dark 0.306) are thin or boundary-like, which is what boundary auxiliary heads target.
- ELPV image labels cover BMRK (our weakest source); UCF-EL's "crack" region is roughly crack OR inactive. Both need a structured loss to be used at all.
- R from MultiSolSegment (EL, 2026): independent per-class channels let a crack crossing a busbar be both, and keep crack counts closer to GT than a softmax tool that splits cracks at busbars.

## Cheapest decisive experiments
House = U-Net++ / ResNet-34, 20k steps. PF = encoder-preserving variant. Seed rule: 5 seeds per arm against the existing 5-seed house / PF numbers; for inactive also module-grouped 5-fold CV on train + val (test inactive is decided by 3 cells). About 1.1 GPU-h per run.

1. Region / multi-label head (rows 1, 2, 16). Arm: PF + 3 sigmoid channels (D, I, C), BCE + soft Dice, overlap targets, outer-to-inner write order. Baseline: PF. Metrics: inactive IoU (5-fold CV and clean 49), crack IoU, relF1@2, E1 inactive IoU. Kill: inactive CV IoU gain < 0.02 and crack IoU drop > 0.012 vs PF. About 6 GPU-h (5 seeds) + 6 GPU-h CV.
2. Image-level head and gate (rows 3, 4). Arm A: PF + 2 presence heads, soft gate. Arm B: arm A + ELPV image labels. Baseline: PF. Metrics: inactive precision and IoU, BMRK crack IoU, E1 inactive IoU. Kill: inactive CV IoU gain < 0.02 and BMRK crack IoU gain < 0.02. About 12 GPU-h.
3. Boundary auxiliary head (row 5, then row 6 only if positive). Arm: house + SBCB head, dropped at inference. Baseline: house. Metrics: gridline, edge_dark, material IoU, mIoU_rob. Kill: mIoU_rob gain < 0.01 and no weak class up by >= 0.03. About 6 GPU-h.
4. Distance auxiliary (row 7). Arm: house + truncated distance-to-crack regression head (L1, weight 0.5). Baseline: house. Metrics: crack relF1@2, crack IoU, E1 relF1@2. Kill: relF1@2 gain < 0.01 on test and on E1. About 6 GPU-h.
5. Partial-label joint training with UCF-EL (rows 8, 9). Arm: PF trained jointly (1:1 batches) with UCF-EL under the union loss. Baseline: PF. Metrics: E1 crack recall and relF1@2, inactive IoU. Kill: E1 relF1@2 gain < 0.02 or test crack IoU drop > 0.012. About 10 GPU-h.
6. Tree loss (row 12, then row 11). Arm: house + Wasserstein label-tree loss. Baseline: house. Metrics: gridline, splice, edge_dark IoU, mIoU_rob. Kill: mIoU_rob gain < 0.01 and splice still 0.000. About 6 GPU-h.
7. Constraint loss (rows 10, 13). Arm: best of 1 / 2 + exclusion and adjacency violation loss. Metrics: inactive precision; count of predicted inactive regions with no crack within 5 px. Kill: inactive IoU gain < 0.01 over its base. About 6 GPU-h.

## Overlap with BRIEF section 5 (honest)
- Multi-label heads, region outputs, image-level heads, boundary / distance auxiliary heads, hierarchical heads and tree losses, logic or topology-interaction constraints: none run. Section 5 lists multi-label heads and edge / centerline formulations as never tried.
- UCF-EL was used only as encoder pretraining that the house fine-tune erased; joint training with a partial-label loss is open.
- ELPV image labels: listed as unused data.
- Skeleton Recall and clDice (section 5) are single-class crack topology losses; they do not test cross-class constraints.
- Offset-tolerant CE (section 5, n.d.) is a crack loss; the distance-regression head (row 7) is a separate output and target, not the same mechanism.
- Sibling directions D01 / D03 also list MultiSolSegment and STC-Net; experiment 1 here should be the single shared run.

## Rejected (one line each)
- arXiv 2104.02745 InverseForm: penalises small boundary shifts; our 1 to 2 px crack jitter turns that signal into noise.
- arXiv 2503.23519 BoundMatch: boundary multi-task inside teacher-student SSL; SSL at 100% labels was n.d. here; its useful part is row 5 (same authors).
- arXiv 2007.04269 SegFix: boundary-to-interior offset refinement; helps blobs, not 1 to 2 px cracks; belongs to D26.
- arXiv 1705.07115 Kendall uncertainty weighting: a loss-balancing tool for the arms above, not a mechanism for our gaps; use only if hand weights fail.
- arXiv 1907.05740 Gated-SCNN and arXiv 2007.10035 DecoupleSegNets: heavier versions of the boundary / body-edge idea in rows 5 and 6; run only if those show a gain.
- doi:10.1109/cvpr52733.2024.02666 Poincare-ball hierarchical segmentation: hyperbolic embeddings for deep taxonomies; our tree has 2 levels and 24 leaves, rows 11 and 12 test the same idea more cheaply.
- doi:10.1007/978-3-031-72111-3_68 multi-class Betti matching: persistent-homology loss per class, costly at 512 px; topology losses belong to D10.
- doi:10.1155/2023/3301106 two-stream boundary-aware concrete crack network and doi:10.1016/j.autcon.2024.105354 steel crack boundary guidance: crack-only boundary streams; for a 1 to 2 px label the boundary is the crack, so they reduce to row 5 / D09 architectures.
- doi:10.1109/tip.2023.3290519 Conditional Boundary Loss: a loss, covered by D18.
- arXiv 2511.15603 MaskMed and other mask-classification decoders: covered by D23.

## Gaps
- No 2025 to 2026 paper found that measures region-based (nested sigmoid) training against softmax on 2D thin structures; the only fresh comparison (row 16) is 3D brain lesions and is mixed.
- No paper found that encodes an adjacency (not containment) constraint between classes; the "inactive touches crack" rule has to be built by us (rows 10, 13 give the machinery).
- Semantic Scholar was rate-limited the whole session, so search_snippets evidence queries (sigmoid vs softmax on overlapping thin classes) did not run, and Semantic Scholar-only papers may be missing.
- Code for Bevandic 2022, MultiTalent, DDT, Label tree losses and the Hijjawi benchmark not verified. MultiSolSegment has no public code found.
- Panoptic / instance treatment of cracks for severity (per-crack length, mode, inactive fraction) deserves its own direction; row 15 is only a start.
