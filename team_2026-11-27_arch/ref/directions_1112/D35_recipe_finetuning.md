## 1. Why this direction matters here (E, from BRIEF)
- The house fine-tune (Adam 1e-3, wd 1e-4, cosine, 20k steps, batch 8) drives ResNet-34 layer3 / layer4 to cosine about 0 against the ImageNet init. PF (encoder frozen 1k steps, then 0.1x lr, no encoder wd) keeps them and lifts E1 inactive IoU 0.05 to 0.24, test n.d. to a narrow relF1@2 gain.
- So "how far the encoder moves" is a proven lever on E1 (problem 3). PF is one crude point on that axis. Optimizer, weight-decay form, weight averaging, flatness, normalisation, sampling and schedule length were never varied (BRIEF section 5, optimizer row: "AdamW, layer-wise lr decay, SAM optimizer, EMA / SWA, longer schedules; never varied").
- Caveat (R, arxiv:2002.11770): regularisation toward the init "does not necessarily apply for dissimilar datasets". EL is far from ImageNet. Our PF result says preservation helped on E1 anyway.
- Most arms below are cheap (one 1.1 GPU-h run per seed) and several are free (post hoc on existing checkpoints). They can also be stacked onto any later architecture.

## 4. Key evidence (R)
- ERM++ (full text, Table 2a, ResNet-50, 5 DomainBed datasets): full recipe 68.9 avg. Removing MPA 65.9 (-3.0, largest), removing full data 67.7, removing strong init 67.6, removing unfrozen BN 68.3, removing Auto-LR 68.4, removing warm start 68.7.
- Crack sampling (full text, Crack500 full-res, U-Net from scratch, AdamW 3e-4, 20k updates, batch 16, BCE + Dice, flips + rot90 only, 3 paired seeds): AP at 2 px tolerance, random crops 0.812 (BN), aggressive positive-only 0.789, 10% positive crops 0.730. Positive-only sampling made 47% of image area unreachable. GroupNorm random crops 0.844 vs BatchNorm 0.812 (+0.032). The recipe is close to ours (CE / BCE + Dice, label-safe aug, 20k steps), which is why this is ranked high.
- SPD (full text, Table 4, PASCAL-Context Swin-T, ID / OOD mIoU): vanilla FT 66.03 / 44.00, L2-SP 73.47 / 56.70, LLRD 72.09 / 54.47, FTP 73.79 / 58.45, Adam-SPD 74.27 / 60.06.
- PGPS (abstract): +1.28% relative Dice over 15 3D tasks at equal FLOPs, better on all 15, largest on lesion tasks with severe imbalance, lower seed variance.
- Episodic sampling (abstract): low-data Dice 0.787 vs 0.758 / 0.762, but driven by 12x more iterations; under matched budgets the rest overfit earlier.

## 5. Proposed experiment plan (S)
All arms use SUP100-PF (current best) or the house model as stated, seeds 5 to 9, 20k steps unless stated, same commit and data. Select on val / F51 / 5-fold CV; open test and E1 only at a pre-registered point.
Co-primaries: test clean crack relF1@2, inactive IoU, mIoU_rob; E1 crack relF1@2, E1 inactive IoU.

- Stage 0, zero training (existing checkpoints): precise-BN recompute; WiSE-FT and LiNeS on house s5-9 and PF s5-9. Kill: no coefficient beats both endpoints on val by +0.005 relF1@2.
- Stage 1, in-run averaging (one run gives raw and averaged weights): EMA, SWAD, ERM++ MPA. Kill: averaged minus raw < +0.005 val relF1@2 paired over 5 seeds and E1 inactive not better.
- Stage 2, normalisation: decoder GroupNorm (row 3), frozen encoder BN (row 16). Kill: crack relF1@2 and inactive IoU both within noise (0.012) of PF.
- Stage 3, feature-preserving regularisers vs PF: L2-SP, SPD, FD + RCS. Kill: no co-primary better and per-layer cosine to init not above PF's (mechanism check).
- Stage 4, optimizer and schedule: AdamW x lr x LLRD grid and surgical freezes on 5-fold CV (2 seeds per cell); schedule length 20k / 80k / 160k with EMA; PGPS; episodic batches. The CV grid doubles as a better instrument for problem 5.
- Stage 5, flatness: SAM (2x cost), then SAGM / DGSAM / Focal-SAM only if SAM moves E1 crack relF1@2 by +0.02 or test crack relF1@2 by +0.01.
- Final: stack the winners (for example SPD + EMA/MPA + GN decoder), retrain on all 532 cells with the CV-fixed schedule (ERM++ full-data step), 5 seeds.
Total: about 300 to 400 GPU-h, 3 to 4 days on 8 A6000.

## 7. Gaps
- No paper studies schedule length for small-data 2D CNN segmentation fine-tuning directly. Row 15 is 3D MRI and its numbers come from a secondary summary. The arm must be run.
- No augmentation study for 1 to 2 px jittered labels was found in two passes. A "label-safe intensity policy" arm (gamma, blur sigma below 1 px, Gaussian / Poisson noise, local contrast, all without resampling the mask) stays untested and was not found in the literature. Sub-pixel geometric augmentation (small rotations, scaling) needs mask resampling that thickens or breaks crack labels; this needs its own design.
- Crop / multi-scale: native-resolution crops for the 1200 px pool sources are not covered here (D22). Row 3 says crops must keep the full image reachable.
- search_snippets never worked, so per-paper segmentation numbers for SWAD, EMA, LiNeS and SAM on dense tasks were not pulled.
- Code for L2-SP, EMA, WiSE-FT, surgical FT is not needed (a few lines each). SPD, SWAD, ERM++ repos were confirmed only through aggregators or the paper, not by opening GitHub.
