# D38 In-context, few-shot and promptable segmentation as a labeller

Scout: D38, 13 Nov 2026. R = what a paper measured, E = our files, S = my judgement.

## Bottom line (S)
- No paper applies in-context or promptable few-shot segmentation to EL, or shows it working on 1 to 2 px structures. The closest evidence is industrial defects (GFDS, Liu 2025): at 1-shot, SAM2 used in video-tracking mode (support = previous frame) scores 47.9 mIoU over 54 defect categories at 1024 px, but only 38.8 at 512 px; PerSAM 8.0 and Matcher 5.0 (R).
- SAM-family decoders predict masks at 1/4 of the input size. A 1.75 px crack at 512 px is under 1 px at that grid even after upsampling to 1024. So as a crack labeller these tools are weak by construction; their realistic value is for inactive (blobby), the structural classes (splice, ribbons, busbars, edge_dark) and coarse crack detection on new contexts.
- We are not in a few-shot regime: 478 labelled cells. In-context labellers can only help where we lack labels: half-cut cells / E1-like modules (0 train half-cuts), the 1,152 multi half-cells in the pool, and rare classes. SemiSAM+ (R) itself reports that generalist-model supervision adds little once labels are plentiful.
- Cheapest decisive gate for every labeller arm: score the labeller's own predictions on held-out labelled cells we already have (val 54, F51, H36 inactive) against the house PF ensemble. A labeller that is not better than, or at least decorrelated from, the house teacher cannot help distillation. Only arms that pass the gate get a training run.
- The strongest non-labeller idea in this direction is a retrieval (kNN) head (Hummingbird): fuse house logits with label votes from nearest labelled patches. It targets rare classes and needs no new training.
- For the benchmark, a "K-shot new-module" track (the approved T3b E1 few-shot track) needs in-context baselines: SAM2 video mode, SANSA, SegGPT, FSS-SAM3 (gated), DINOv3 prototypes, plus the house PF fine-tuned on the same K cells. Support selection must be fixed in the protocol (retrieval rule and K), because prompt choice moves VICL scores a lot (R, Zhang 2023).

## Minimum experiments (arm, baseline, metric, kill line)
All on dev data only (val 54, F51, H36 inactive, CV5 out-of-fold); E1 stays sealed until the T3b track runs.
- G0 labeller gate (covers #1, #2, #3, #6, #10, #11): each labeller with K = 1, 4, 16 retrieved supports from train predicts val 54 + F51 + H36. Baseline: house PF 5-seed ensemble predictions on the same cells. Metrics: inactive IoU, crack relF1@2, splice / gridline IoU, plus error correlation with the house (fraction of house misses the labeller recovers). Kill: labeller below the house on every class AND recovers under 10% of house misses on H36 inactive. Cost: 2 to 10 GPU-h per labeller.
- #1 / #2 / #13 distillation (only after G0 pass): PF trained on 478 + pool half-cell pseudo-labels from the passing labeller (with #13 one-sided loss) vs PF trained on 478 + the same half-cells pseudo-labelled by the house ensemble (separates "FM labeller" from "more half-cut data"). Metric: H36 inactive IoU and F51 crack relF1@2, then E1 in T3b. Kill: under margin M on both. About 2 GPU-h per seed, 2 seeds screen.
- #8 retrieval head: house PF fold models on cv5v2 with and without kNN fusion (memory from the fold's train cells, same-source and any-source variants). Metric: out-of-fold mIoU_rob, splice / gridline / inactive IoU. Kill: mIoU_rob gain under margin M and no class above +0.02. CPU / 1 GPU-h, no training.
- #5 module-as-video: OFL-SAM2 style on pool / H36 cells ordered by module, vs SAM2 video mode with fixed supports only (#1). Metric: H36 inactive IoU. Kill: no gain over #1.
- #4 / #3 SAM3 (needs the user's OK for gated weights): FS-CPL prompt per class learned on 478 cells vs FSS-SAM3 exemplar canvas, both vs house PF on val + F51. Kill: under house PF on inactive and crack relF1@2.
- #12 EI-AE: only if code is found; train on 3k defect-free pool cells, residual map as a 2nd input channel to house PF. Metric: CV5 crack relF1@2 and F51 crack recall. Kill: under margin M.
- Benchmark K-shot track: for K in {1, 4, 16} same-source supports, rows #1, #2, #6, #10, #7, plus house PF fine-tuned on the K cells (the parametric baseline). Support retrieval rule fixed (#15).

## Gaps
- No paper tests in-context / few-shot FM segmentation on 1 to 2 px structures; search_snippets (Semantic Scholar) was rate-limited on all 3 tries, so failure evidence on thin structures was not collected.
- Hummingbird official code: not found; a kNN head is simple to write ourselves.
- EI-AE code and FS-CPL / OFL-SAM2 / SemiSAM+ repos: unverified.
- SAM3 weights are gated (user's OK needed); DINOv3 too.
- No labelled half-cut cells with reliable crack labels exist (H36 crack labels are specks), so new-context crack labelling cannot be validated before E1; only inactive and structural classes can be checked on H36.
- Sub-direction worth its own search: retrieval-augmented segmentation heads (kNN memory fused with a parametric model) for long-tail classes.
