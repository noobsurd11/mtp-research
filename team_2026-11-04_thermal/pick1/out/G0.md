## Rules applied (frozen)
- Cell features: ΔT = median T over inner 80% of the cell minus the module median of cell medians; EL dark fraction = share of inner-90% pixels < 0.5 × module median of cell medians, after an opening (radius 2% of cell side). Positive ("EL-dark cell") = dark fraction ≥ 5%.
- QA: module excluded if EL or IR registration RMS residual > 5% of a cell side.
- Primary orientation: rot180 (A1 from the authors' crop transform; A2 by same lab / rig, S). Go: AUROC(cooler) ≥ 0.8. Null: |ρ| < 0.3 and AUROC < 0.7. Kill of the fusion / weak-label families needs a null in the sunlit A3 arm too (pending).

**POST HOC reading (not a verdict change; first computed by check after the primary readout, factcheck §22 G0-3). Where the dark-IR signal sits:** subtracting each row's median within a module makes all orientations agree on A2 (ρ −0.42 rot180, −0.44 flipv, −0.46 id), so the orientation dependence is entirely top-bottom (row-level) structure. Subtracting row and column medians removes the signal everywhere (A2 ρ −0.07 to 0.00, AUROC 0.49-0.52; A1 ρ −0.17 to +0.05, AUROC 0.52-0.57). So the signal is carried by row patterns and left-right-symmetric column patterns, not by single cells (S). That fits real string / edge-row degradation, but equally fits shared artefacts: a convection gradient plus an EL top-bottom gradient, and frame heat-sinking plus vignetting at the edges (S). No verdict change.
