## 3. Pick 3: A1 arm stopped at T37 (team-lead option A)
- **T15 PASS, narrow:** robust SD of the cell deficit 3.26 mV vs the 2.57 mV line; 5,688 / 5,688 cells valid (E, recomputed independently). **T37 v1** 3 / 10 excluded (30%, at the line), with a visual misregistration of an included module. **T37 v2 FAIL:** 4 / 10 excluded by the frozen 5% rule (E). T02 / T12 not run.
- Why it stopped:
  - the label is a diffuse small resistive deficit (p90 4.23 mV, ~1.18× EL dimming), not inactive area (E; A1 losses "primarily resistive", R: Colvin 2023);
  - its direction may be inverted: each cell's voltage comes from "a bright pixel", and bright-spot cells have higher RS (R: Colvin 2023 §II.A, §III.B);
  - IR registration root cause: the authors' IR crop is a fixed 134 × 35 window that trims ~0.9 cell of the module (E), so the fix for any future A1 work is raw-frame registration;
  - dark IR tracks rows and edges (E, pick 1).
- **Redirect:** pick3b on A3 (`pick3/plan_pick3b.md` v1, eaec6f26, frozen 13:43 before A3 extraction).
  - **Label:** the pre-registered label was STC I-V power loss against nameplate. Discovery (structure only, after the freeze) found no module type in the release (names, README, I-V files with V and I columns only). So under the frozen fallback, **FF loss** (1 − FF / median FF over non-soiling modules) is the primary label (E).
  - **Readouts:** 3b-R1, does the EL dark proxy track FF loss; 3b-R2, does drone IR track FF loss and add to EL; 3b-R3, cell-level IR on interconnect-disconnection modules, using pick 1's QA-passing cells (same Otsu IR outline, pick 1 Amendment 5).
